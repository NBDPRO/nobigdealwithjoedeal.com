This is how the check above weighs things, and it’s the same way I think about it on a roof. Count which column most of your answers land in.

Likely a repair

  • The roof is under 10–15 years old
  • The problem is in one spot: a few blown-off shingles, a cracked pipe boot, one flashing leak
  • No repairs before, or just one
  • You’re selling soon and the rest of the roof is sound

Worth an inspection to decide

  • The roof is 15–20 years old, or you don’t know
  • Problems on one side or slope, not everywhere
  • A leak you can’t trace to one obvious spot
  • Soft or sagging spots: that’s the decking under the shingles, and it needs eyes on it

Likely nearing replacement

  • The roof is 20+ years old
  • Curling, cracking or granule loss across several slopes
  • Two or more repairs already, and new problems keep showing up
  • You plan to stay 10+ years, so fixing it once beats patching it again
