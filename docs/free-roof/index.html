NBD Gives Back

One Free Roof a Year.
This Is It.

Nominate a neighbor. Or nominate yourself.

Every year, I pick one Cincinnati-area homeowner and give them a complete roof replacement — free. No strings. No pitch. Just a new roof from a local crew that actually cares who's under it.

Open Now Cincinnati-Area Homeowners One Entry Per Person
Who It's For

If you know someone who needs a roof and can't get one — tell me.

I'm not looking for a sob story. I'm looking for a real homeowner whose roof is failing and replacing it isn't on the table. Veterans, widows, single parents on a fixed income, neighbors whose insurance won't cover it — anyone whose life would genuinely change with a roof that won't leak anymore. Nominate yourself, or nominate someone whose story you know.

Veterans & Military Families

Active duty, retired, Guard, or a surviving spouse. If you served, you move to the front of the line.

Widows & Single Parents

One income, a house to keep up, and a roof that's been patched one too many times. I know the math.

Fixed Income & Disability

Social Security, VA disability, or any situation where a $15k roof replacement isn't happening without help.

Insurance Wouldn't Pay

Your carrier denied a legitimate claim, or the damage fell through the cracks. I've been on that call.

How It Works

Four steps, one winner, every year.

Submit an Entry

Takes three minutes. You can nominate yourself or a neighbor, family member, or friend.

I Read Every One

No algorithm. I'm the one reading submissions. If I have questions, I'll call.

Finalists Get an Inspection

I come out, climb the roof, and confirm what it needs. Free regardless of whether you're selected.

Winner Announced

Drawing date posted on NBD socials. Full replacement — tear-off, new shingles, warranty — on the house.

The Fine Print

Rules, honestly stated.

  • Open to homeowners in NBD's Greater Cincinnati service area (Hamilton, Clermont, Warren, Butler, Campbell, Kenton, Boone counties).
  • One entry per household. Duplicate submissions are consolidated, not disqualified.
  • Nominee must be the legal homeowner of the property at the time of installation.
  • Finalists' roof condition is confirmed by a free NBD inspection. The inspection happens regardless of whether you're selected.
  • Winner is selected at NBD's sole discretion. This is a gift, not a contest — I'm looking for real need, not the best essay.
  • Full replacement covers materials, labor, tear-off, underlayment, flashing, and a standard NBD warranty. Structural damage beyond a normal replacement is discussed ahead of work.
  • If the selected home is structurally unsound or insured for a covered replacement already, NBD may select a different finalist.
  • NBD reserves the right to document the gift on social media. The homeowner's privacy preferences come first — faces and exact addresses only shared with explicit consent.
