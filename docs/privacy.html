Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy. Here's exactly how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last Updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to nobigdealwithjoedeal.com (the "Website"), operated by No Big Deal Home Solutions, a home services contractor serving Greater Cincinnati, Ohio and Northern Kentucky. By using this Website and providing your information, you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide Directly

When you submit a free estimate request, contact form, or communicate with us, we collect:

  • Name, phone number, email address
  • Physical address or zip code
  • Description of your project or service needed
  • Any other information you voluntarily provide

Free Tools and Interactive Features

Our free homeowner tools collect contact information as part of how they work:

  • Roof Score: your quiz answers are scored in your browser; to receive the detailed report, you provide your name, phone number, and (optionally) email, which we store as a lead so Joe can follow up.
  • Instant Estimate: the estimate flow collects your name, phone number, email, and property address, and verifies your phone number by sending a one-time code by SMS (through our verification provider, Twilio). We store whether the number was verified along with your estimate request. If you choose "skip verification," we store your request as an unverified callback lead instead.
  • Storm tools and other forms (storm claim check, storm report, storm alerts, roof visualizer, free inspection, contractor guide): where these ask for contact details, we store what you enter as a lead together with the tool you used, so we can respond with the result you requested.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Website, we automatically collect:

  • Browser type and version
  • Operating system
  • IP address
  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring webpage and links clicked
  • Device identifiers and similar technical data

Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from our service providers (see section 5 below) to help us understand how you use our Website and to improve our services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

  • Responding to your estimate requests and inquiries
  • Providing quotes and scheduling consultations
  • Processing payments and managing customer accounts
  • Sending follow-up communications about your project
  • Improving our Website and services
  • Marketing and promotional communications (with your consent)
  • Complying with legal obligations
  • Preventing fraud and ensuring security

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to understand how you use our Website and to improve your experience. Cookies are small files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and analyze usage patterns.

Types of Cookies

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the Website to function properly
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the Website
  • Marketing Cookies: Track your interactions to deliver relevant content

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect Website functionality. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use third-party services to operate and improve our Website. These partners may collect and use your information as described below:

Firebase

We use Google Firebase for data storage, authentication, and database management. Firebase securely stores customer inquiry data and project information. Firebase is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics (Measurement ID: G-8PG7N9Q3DL) to track Website traffic and user behavior. This helps us understand what's working and improve your experience. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect data and is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.

Microsoft Clarity

We may use Microsoft Clarity on our public Website pages to see, in aggregate, how visitors use them (where people click, how far they scroll, which pages confuse them) through heatmaps and anonymized session replays. Clarity captures mouse movement, clicks, scrolls and page interactions; text you type into forms is masked and never sent. Clarity is not used in our customer portal, CRM, or any page that shows your account or project details. It does not run if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. Microsoft and its affiliates may use this data as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

Stripe

When you make a payment, we use Stripe to process credit card and payment information. Stripe handles all payment processing and does not store your full payment details on our servers. Stripe is PCI compliant and subject to Stripe's Privacy Policy.

Email & Communication Services

We use Resend to deliver transactional email (estimate confirmations, appointment reminders, receipts) and Twilio to send SMS messages when you have opted in. These providers process your email address, phone number, and message content on our behalf.

AI-Assisted Features

Some features (property intelligence, proposal drafting, objection handling assistance) use the Anthropic API (Claude). When you interact with AI features we may send the addresses, notes, or questions you submit to Anthropic for processing. Anthropic does not use this data to train their models under our API agreement.

E-Signature

Contracts and documents we send for signature are signed on our own e-signature system, which stores them with the rest of our records on Google Cloud (Firebase). To let you sign, we use your name, email address, and the document. When you sign or decline, we record your consent to sign electronically, the date and time, your IP address, your browser and device information, and a fingerprint (SHA-256 hash) of the document; that record is kept with the signed copy as evidence of the signature. Signing links, reminders, and your signed copy are emailed to you through Resend.

Roof Measurements

Aerial roof measurement reports are sourced from the providers below. To produce a report we share the property address, or its map coordinates, with the chosen provider; in return we receive roof dimensions and imagery. No personal information beyond the property location is transmitted, except that a human-certified report order may carry the homeowner's name so the provider's drawing team can identify the job.

  • Instant Roofer (primary — AI measurements and human-certified reports)
  • HOVER (fallback)
  • EagleView (fallback)
  • Nearmap (fallback)

Property Intelligence

Public county records, ownership data, and parcel shapes are looked up through Regrid or Swath API. Only the property address is shared to perform these lookups.

Weather & Storm Data

Historical hail reports and storm prediction data are sourced from the free National Weather Service / NOAA feeds and, optionally, from HailTrace or Swath API (paid/metered tiers). Only approximate property coordinates are shared.

Appointment Scheduling

When you book an inspection through our homeowner portal, Cal.com processes your name, email address, phone number, and selected time slot on our behalf.

Security & Monitoring

We use Sentry for error monitoring and Cloudflare Turnstile to distinguish humans from bots on public forms. Sentry receives anonymized error events (PII such as email and phone is stripped before submission). Turnstile uses a privacy-preserving challenge and does not track you across sites. Turnstile runs in invisible mode on our public forms, so you will not see or need to complete a challenge; its processing of your data is governed by the Cloudflare Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

Voice & Map Lookups

If you leave a voice message or use a call-recording feature we offer, the audio is transcribed by Groq (primary) or, as a fallback, Deepgram. We use Google Maps and, for parcel/address lookups, OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service and U.S. Census geocoding — all three receive only the address or coordinates needed to complete the lookup. Upstash provides backend rate-limiting infrastructure and does not receive personal information beyond a hashed identifier used to count requests.

Phone Calls: Thursday, Our Automated Phone Assistant

When you call our business line and our automated phone assistant ("Thursday") answers, the call is handled by Bland AI. The call is recorded and transcribed. We store the recording, the transcript, and a summary of the call in our records, and create a lead and follow-up task from it. To pull the details of your request (such as your name, address, and what you need) out of the conversation, we send the transcript, the call summary, and your phone number to Anthropic (Claude), described under AI-Assisted Features above.

Recorded Business Calls

Calls to and from our owner's business phone may be recorded by a call-recording app on that phone. Recordings are saved to his Google Drive and copied into our private storage, where the audio is transcribed by Groq and the transcript is summarized by Anthropic (Claude) to create notes and follow-up reminders on the matching customer record. If a call is identified as personal rather than business, its transcript is not stored and our stored copy of the audio is deleted.

Roof Visualizer

If you use the roof and siding visualizer on our Website, the house photo you upload and the materials and colors you choose are sent to an AI image-generation provider — Replicate, or Kie.ai as an alternate — to produce the preview image, and to Anthropic (Claude) to write the assessment shown with it. You do not need an account to use the visualizer.

AI Assistants with Access to Our CRM

We use AI assistant bots built on xAI's Grok to help run the business. They connect to our customer records through a key limited to our own company's data. They can read customer names, property addresses, project stage and value, follow-up dates, recent notes and reminders, our schedule, and estimates; phone numbers, email addresses, and insurance claim and policy numbers are never provided to them. They can only file notes, reminders, and reports for our owner to review — they cannot send messages, take payments, or change or delete customer records. Information they read is processed by xAI.

Thumbtack

If you request a quote through Thumbtack, Thumbtack forwards your name, contact information, and project details to us so we can respond to your request.

We do not sell, share, or rent your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes. Our third-party partners may use your information only to perform services on our behalf and are bound by confidentiality agreements.

NBD Pro — Software Service

NBD Pro is our software product for other roofing and home-service contractors ("NBD Pro Customers"). Where this Website is concerned, we are the party responsible for your data — the "controller," in privacy terminology. Where a contractor uses NBD Pro to manage their own customers' information, that contractor is the controller of their customers' data, and we act only as a "processor" on their behalf, under the terms of their NBD Pro subscription agreement. If you are the customer of an NBD Pro Customer (for example, you booked a roofing job with a contractor who uses NBD Pro), your privacy request should generally go to that contractor first; we assist them in fulfilling it. The service providers named throughout this section (Firebase, Stripe, Twilio, Anthropic, Resend, Groq, Deepgram, Google Maps, Regrid/Swath, NOAA/HailTrace, Cal.com, Sentry, Cloudflare Turnstile, Upstash, Thumbtack, Bland AI, Replicate, Kie.ai, and xAI) process NBD Pro data under the same terms described above.

5. Text Messages, Calls, and TCPA Compliance

If you provide your phone number, you may receive text messages or calls from No Big Deal Home Solutions about your estimate request, project updates, or follow-up communications.

Phone verification codes: some tools (such as the Instant Estimate) send a one-time verification code by SMS to confirm the phone number you entered is yours. These codes are sent through Twilio, are used only to verify your number, and are separate from any marketing communications.

TCPA Notice: By providing your phone number and submitting an estimate request, you consent to receive SMS text messages and phone calls from No Big Deal Home Solutions at the number you provided, regarding your home service estimate and project. Standard message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to any text message to opt out of SMS communications. You can also call us at (859) 420-7382 to update your preferences.

We comply with the Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA) and will not send marketing texts or calls without your explicit consent. You may withdraw consent at any time.

Your mobile information: No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Text message terms

  • Who texts you and why: No Big Deal Home Solutions texts homeowners who give us their number and agree to texts, about their estimate or inspection request, appointments, their project, and invoices and payments. If you sign up on our Storm Alerts page, we also text you when severe weather is reported near your zip code.
  • How often: message frequency varies. Most people get a few texts while their request or project is open. Storm alerts are usually 2–4 texts per season.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply.
  • Stopping and help: reply STOP to any text to opt out, and you will get one text confirming it. Reply HELP for help, or call (859) 420-7382.
  • Agreeing to texts is not a condition of any purchase. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

The full text message program terms, including how to opt in, opt out and get help, are on our Terms & Conditions page.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our services, fulfill legal obligations, and maintain business records.

  • Contact inquiries: Retained for 3 years after the estimate request
  • Payment records: Retained for 7 years for tax and accounting purposes
  • Marketing communications: Retained until you unsubscribe
  • Analytics data: Typically retained for 12 months

If you wish to delete your information, contact us at jd@nobigdealwithjoedeal.com or (859) 420-7382. We will delete or anonymize your data within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or business purposes.

7. Data Security

We take the security of your personal information seriously. We implement industry-standard security measures including:

  • Encryption of data in transit (HTTPS/SSL)
  • Secure data storage in Firebase
  • Restricted access to customer data
  • Regular security updates and monitoring
  • Secure payment processing through Stripe (PCI compliant)

While we use reasonable security measures, no online system is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your information. Please use strong passwords and do not share your login credentials.

8. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

Access and Correction

You have the right to access, review, and update your personal information. Contact us at jd@nobigdealwithjoedeal.com to request your data.

Opt-Out of Communications

You can opt out of marketing emails by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email. You can opt out of text messages by replying STOP. We will still send you transactional messages about your estimate or project unless you request to stop all communications.

Do Not Track

Some browsers can send a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. Google Analytics on our Website does not currently respond to these signals. Microsoft Clarity, where we use it, does not load when your browser sends either signal (see Section 4). You can also limit tracking through your browser settings and cookie controls.

California Privacy Rights (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have the right to know what personal information is collected, to delete your information, to opt out of the "sale" of your information, and to non-discrimination for exercising your rights. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Other State Privacy Laws

Similar rights may apply under privacy laws in Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut, Utah, and other states. We will honor requests under applicable state privacy laws.

9. Children's Privacy

Our Website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided personal information, we will delete that information immediately. Parents who believe their child has submitted information should contact us at jd@nobigdealwithjoedeal.com.

10. External Links

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. We encourage you to review their privacy policies before submitting any information.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Your continued use of the Website following changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
