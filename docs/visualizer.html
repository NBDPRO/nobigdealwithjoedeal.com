Know the Brand? Use Theirs First.

Every visualizer — mine and the manufacturers' — is a simulation. The colors and textures are generated, so they'll vary with the light, your screen and your actual house. If you already know the brand, the manufacturer's own tool gives you a better shot at an accurate look:
