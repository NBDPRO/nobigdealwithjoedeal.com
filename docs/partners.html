Why It Works

What You're Actually Handing Off

When you refer somebody to a big outfit, a salesperson shows up on commission, and how that goes is out of your hands. When you refer somebody to me, I show up. Same person who inspects the roof, writes the estimate, and runs the job.

If there's no damage, I tell them there's no damage. No manufactured urgency, no "today only" pricing, no pressure to sign on the hood of a truck. Your name stays clean either way — which is the only thing that makes a referral relationship worth having.

Questions

Common Ones

What area does No Big Deal Home Solutions cover?
The shop is in Goshen and I'm based out of Eastgate, but the work spans Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky — Clermont, Hamilton, Warren, Butler, and Brown counties in Ohio, plus Kenton, Campbell, and Boone counties in Kentucky. If you're not sure whether an address is in range, just ask.
How are subcontractor crews paid?
Per square on install work, with scope and price agreed in writing before anybody gets on a roof. No surprise backcharges. Certificates of insurance have to be current before the first job — that part isn't flexible.
Do you white-label or subcontract for other contractors?
Depends on the job. I've worked both sides of that arrangement. Reach out with the specifics and I'll tell you honestly whether it's a fit.
Do you offer local sponsorships?
Yes. Youth sports, school programs, trade and robotics programs, church and neighborhood events across Clermont County and Greater Cincinnati are all considered.
How much do you sponsor for?
It varies with the season and what's being asked. I'm a one-man shop, not a franchise with a marketing budget, so I'd rather do a few things meaningfully than write fifty small checks. Send me what you're putting together and I'll give you a straight yes or no.
