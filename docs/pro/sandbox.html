Follow one roof job from the storm to paid in full.

Nine short steps through the real NBD Pro screens, filled with a made-up company and customer. Nothing you tap is saved or sent.

9 steps

Step 1 · Storm

Hail hits near your customers.

Storm Center turns a weather alert into a zone and shows who is inside it. Tap the green door.

In the app: Storm Watch pulls live National Weather Service alerts, and every door you knock gets one of 16 outcomes, from Appointment Set to Do Not Knock.

Step 2 · Lead

The knock becomes a lead.

An Appointment Set at the door lands as a lead with the time booked. Nothing to retype.

In the app: the card moves itself when the work happens, from meeting booked to paid in full, and every move is logged on the customer’s card.

Step 3 · Photos and report

Inspection photos become a homeowner report.

Tag photos on the roof, then flip to what the homeowner gets.

In the app: photos are marked on-site when taken within 75 m of the address, AI suggests the tags, and reports come from four templates.

Step 4 · Estimate

Good, better, best in one tap.

Pick a package the way the homeowner would. Prices here are samples.

In the app: up to five packages, Economy to Beyond, from one set of measurements. Homeowners see retail prices only, and you get an alert when they open it.

Step 5 · AI bots and the Agent inbox

A bot drafts the follow-up. You decide.

Edit the draft if you like, then tap Text from my phone. Nothing sends on its own.

In the app (paid plans): bots read your CRM and file drafts, notes and reminders into your Agent inbox. None of them can text, email or charge anyone. Texting from NBD Pro itself: coming soon.

Step 6 · E-sign

Signed on their phone, Kentucky rules built in.

A Kentucky insurance job: nothing is due at signing. Flip to Retail to compare.

A retail (cash) job: a normal deposit at signing. Flip back to compare.

In the app: every contract carries the Notice of Right to Cancel and the cancellation forms, links expire after 14 days, and each signature records consent, IP, browser and time.

Step 7 · Production

Crew on the calendar.

Send the sub a job sheet, or push a rain day.

In the app: a production strip for every signed job, a sub roster that warns about expired insurance certificates, and job sheets with no prices or homeowner phone.

Step 8 · Invoice

Invoice from the estimate in one tap.

The pay link stays locked until the insurer’s written decision plus 5 business days.

The deposit was paid at signing; the balance is due on completion.

In the app: online card payments are 3.4% + 30¢ each, paid to your own bank through Stripe. Recording checks and cash is always free.

Step 9 · Paid

Paid in full.

Record the last check with a photo of it. The job closes itself.

In the app: part payments keep the balance right to the cent, and the review and refer-a-friend asks wait until the job is paid in full.
