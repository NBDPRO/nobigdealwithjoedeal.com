Today

Know who needs you today.

Open the app and Today is one list of the people waiting on you: appointments, calls you owe, promised follow-ups, estimates to follow up, money to collect and stalled leads. Estimates that have been out 2, 5 or 10+ days show whether they opened them, and anyone with no next step at all gets flagged.

  • One tap sets the next follow-up: 3 days, a week, two weeks
  • Work them one at a time, a card per screen
  • Counts people, not duplicate cards

One job, start to finish

From the driveway to paid in full.

01 · From the driveway

Quote it before you leave the driveway.

The estimate wizard asks one thing per screen, with a big Next button under your thumb: measure, roof lines, details, penetrations, package, add-ons, photos, review. Five packages, Economy to Beyond.

  • Know when they open it: you get an alert when the homeowner opens the estimate, portal or signing link
  • Homeowners see retail prices only; your costs stay in the CRM

02 · E-sign, built in

Signed on their phone, with the cancel forms attached.

Send for Signature builds the contract from the saved estimate. The homeowner signs on their phone, with no login and no app. Signing links expire after 14 days.

  • Every contract signed in the app carries the Notice of Right to Cancel and two Notice of Cancellation forms, dated the day they sign
  • Up to 4 signers, in order, each with their own link. Reminders go out every 2 days, 3 at most, and a signer can decline
  • Each signature records consent, IP, browser and time, and an audit certificate page is added to the signed PDF

03 · Production

Signed to started, on one strip.

Every signed job gets a production strip: Permit, Ordered, Delivery, Sub, Start. You see what is done and what is next without opening five screens.

  • A sub roster with each crew’s trade, phone and insurance-certificate date. It warns you about an expired certificate or a double-booking
  • Send to sub: a job sheet with the address, start and materials, and no prices or homeowner phone
  • Rain day: push the job and every later one, skipping weekends

04 · Homeowner portal

Your homeowner always knows where it stands.

Send one secure link. The homeowner sees a 9-step tracker (Inspection, Estimate, Signed, Build day set, Build, Final walkthrough, Final payment, Warranty, Review), their estimate, signed documents, photos, and the balance due. No login, no app.

  • The rating and refer-a-friend asks wait until the job is paid in full
  • They can message you, upload their own photos and book a time on your calendar
  • Your company name on every page they see

05 · Getting paid

Get paid without chasing.

Turn the estimate into an invoice in one tap. Log a check, cash, Zelle or a bank transfer with a photo of the proof, or send a card payment link through Stripe that pays out to your own bank. Zelle and check instructions print on the invoice.

  • Part payments keep the balance right to the cent
  • Deposits follow one rule everywhere: none on cash jobs under $2,000, 50% at signing from $2,000 up, and the deductible on insurance jobs (Kentucky is different, see below)
  • Online card payments cost 3.4% + 30¢ each, paid straight to your bank. Recording checks and cash is always free

Your numbers

Know your numbers, honestly.

One close rate everywhere: won ÷ (won + lost), and a signed contract counts as won. Marking a lead lost asks why, so you learn whether it was price, no damage or no response. Sunday Review shows the last 7 days: money collected and still owed, wins, new leads, stuck deals, and the gaps in your own data.

  • Lead sources Track what you spend on Thumbtack, Yelp, Angi and HomeAdvisor next to the jobs they brought. Spend stays owner-only.
  • Six lost reasons Price, went with someone else, no damage, no response, insurance denied, other.
  • On every plan Sunday Review and the close rate are on Free too.

Kentucky

Kentucky‑safe by default.

The rules for insurance jobs are built into the paperwork and the payments, so a rep can’t get them wrong.

  1. No assignment of benefits

    NBD Pro never uses one. The document is retired, and asking for it shows why.

  2. Nothing due at signing

    On a Kentucky insurance job, the deductible and the first insurance payment come due only after the insurer’s written coverage decision and the 5-business-day cancellation window (KRS 367.626).

  3. Pay link stays locked

    The online pay link is refused until then. If a job’s state can’t be read, the hold stays on. Emergency tarp or repair work bills on its own invoice.

  4. The deductible is collected

    Never waived, reduced or absorbed. Nothing NBD Pro prints says otherwise.
