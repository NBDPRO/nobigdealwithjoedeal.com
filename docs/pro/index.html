01 · Pipeline

The job moves itself.

The card moves forward on its own when the work happens: a meeting gets booked, an estimate goes out, a contract is signed, a deposit lands, the job is paid in full. It only moves forward, and it never touches a lost lead.

  • Insurance, cash, finance, warranty and service pipelines, plus a jobs board
  • Every stage change is logged on the customer’s card
  • Swipe a card to the next stage yourself whenever you want

02 · Follow-ups

Know who needs you today.

Home and the pipeline put the people waiting on you first: follow-ups due or overdue, estimates that have been out 2, 5 or 10+ days (and whether they opened them), and anyone with no next step at all.

  • One tap sets the next follow-up: 3 days, a week, two weeks
  • Work them one at a time, a card per screen
  • Counts people, not duplicate cards

03 · Estimates

Quote it from the driveway.

The estimate wizard asks one thing per screen, with a big Next button under your thumb: measure, roof lines, details, penetrations, package, add-ons, photos, review. Five packages, Economy to Beyond.

  • Know when they open it: you get an alert when the homeowner opens the estimate, portal or signing link
  • 25 branded document types: contracts, change orders, warranties, inspection reports
  • The homeowner signs on their phone, with no login and no app. Signing links expire after 14 days
  • Homeowners see retail prices only; your costs stay in the CRM

04 · Getting paid

Get paid without chasing.

Turn the estimate into an invoice in one tap. Log a check, cash, Zelle or a bank transfer with a photo of the proof, or send a card payment link through Stripe that pays out to your own bank. Zelle and check instructions print on the invoice.

  • Part payments keep the balance right to the cent
  • Deposits follow one rule everywhere: none on cash jobs under $2,000, 50% at signing from $2,000 up, and the deductible on insurance jobs (Kentucky is different, see below)
  • Online card payments cost 3.4% + 30¢ each, paid straight to your bank. Recording checks and cash is always free

05 · Homeowner portal

Your homeowner always knows where it stands.

Send one secure link. The homeowner sees a 5-step tracker (Inspection, Estimate, Contract, Installation, Complete), their estimate, signed documents, photos, and the balance due with a Pay Now button once you have sent a payment link. No login, no app.

  • They can message you and upload their own photos
  • They can book a time on your calendar from the page
  • Your company name on every page they see

06 · Storm & door knocking

Storm hits, map’s ready.

Storm Watch pulls live National Weather Service alerts. On the door-knocking map you can show up to five years of hail, wind and tornado reports, draw territories, and log every door with one of 16 outcomes, from Appointment Set to Do Not Knock.

  • The route optimizer turns your not-homes into a walking loop
  • A heat map shows where you have already knocked
  • Team view shows who covered what

Photos, reports and roof drawing.

Shoot from the in-app camera and tag each photo by damage, location and report section. Build a branded report from four templates: Full Roof Inspection, Storm Damage, Supplement and Completion. Draw the roof on satellite imagery when you have no measurement report.

  • Photos file to the customer, ready for the report
  • See at a glance which jobs still need photos
  • Before-and-after photo pairs show up in the homeowner’s portal

10 · Kentucky

Kentucky-safe by default.

The rules for insurance jobs are built into the paperwork and the payments, so a rep can’t get them wrong.

No assignment of benefits

NBD Pro never uses one. The document is retired, and asking for it shows why.

Nothing due at signing

On a Kentucky insurance job, the deductible and the first insurance payment come due only after the insurer’s written coverage decision and the 5-business-day cancellation window (KRS 367.626).

Pay link stays locked

The online pay link is refused until then. If a job’s state can’t be read, the hold stays on. Emergency tarp or repair work bills on its own invoice.

The deductible is collected

Never waived, reduced or absorbed. Nothing NBD Pro prints says otherwise.
