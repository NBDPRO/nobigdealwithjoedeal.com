AI Search Strategy

How Contractors Can Use AI to Get Found in Search (Before Your Competition Does)

Here's something most contractors haven't figured out yet: the way people search is changing. Your future customers aren't just typing "roofer near me" into Google. They're asking ChatGPT "who's the best roofer in Cincinnati?" If you're not visible in AI answers, you're invisible to a growing chunk of your market.

ByJoe Deal ·April 2026·11 min read

What Is GEO (and Why Should You Care)?

GEO = Generative Engine Optimization. It's SEO for AI. When ChatGPT, Perplexity, or Google's AI Overview answers a question, it pulls from websites that follow certain patterns. Those patterns are learnable. And almost no contractors are doing this yet.

This is your first-mover advantage. The contractors who figure this out in 2026 will be citation machines in AI answers by 2027, while their competitors are still wondering why they're invisible.

The 5 GEO Rules for Contractors

1

Question-First Headings

Not: "Our Pricing." Use: "How much does a roof cost in Cincinnati?" AI models look for headings that directly answer questions people are asking. Frame your content as answers, not brags.

2

Direct Answers in First 2 Sentences

Don't bury the lede. AI extracts answers from early in the content. "A roof replacement in Cincinnati costs between $8,500 and $15,000 depending on size and material." Then support it with detail.

3

Cite Real Numbers and Sources

"The average roof replacement in Ohio costs $8,500-$15,000 according to HomeAdvisor 2026 data." AI gives citation credit to sources. If you cite real numbers from real sources, you become a source itself.

4

Use Structured Data (Schema Markup)

Tell Google exactly what your content is. Is it an article? A FAQ? A service page? Schema markup lets search engines (and AI) understand your content better. FAQ schema is especially good for contractor content.

5

Build Your Entity

Consistent NAP (Name, Address, Phone), author bios, about pages, social profiles all connected. AI recognizes entities — trusted, recognized businesses. Make yourself unmistakable as a real, local, credible entity.

The 3-Step GEO Audit You Can Do Right Now

1

Search ChatGPT for Your Services

Ask: "Who's the best roofer in Cincinnati?" or "How do I find a trustworthy contractor for roof replacement?" Are you mentioned? Are competitors? What sources does ChatGPT cite? You're likely not there yet. That's the gap.

2

Check Google's AI Overview

For your key terms, Google shows an AI-generated overview at the top of search. What sources does it cite? What information does it pull? Can you create content that would make that list?

3

Audit Your Own Content

Read your website. Does it answer questions directly or does it bury the answer under fluff? Question-based headings or company-centered ones? Direct answers or buildup? Rewrite for AI.

The Content Repurposing Wheel

One article becomes 6 pieces of content. This is how you build velocity without burning out.

Write one 1,200-word blog post about "How much does a roof replacement cost in Cincinnati?" From that single article, create:

  • The blog post itself
  • A GBP post: "Average roof replacement cost in Cincinnati: $8,500-$15,000. Here's what drives the final price..."
  • Social post 1: "Did you know? A roof replacement in Cincinnati can cost $8,500-$15,000 depending on size and material. Here's the full breakdown..."
  • Social post 2: Quote from the article as a graphic
  • Email to your list: Summary + link to full article
  • Video script: 60-second walkthrough of the pricing breakdown
  • FAQ entry on your site: Condensed version of the article

75-100 articles over 18 months sounds insane. But at 1-2 per week using this repurposing system, it's achievable. And it's how you saturate the AI space.

The Window Is Open (But Closing)

Most contractors don't know GEO is a thing. Most agencies haven't figured it out either. Right now, the contractor who publishes 5-10 well-structured, AI-optimized pieces about their local market becomes THE source that AI models cite.

Six months from now, it'll be harder. A year from now, every contractor will be trying to game it. Be early. Be the source.
