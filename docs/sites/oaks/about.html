Who We Are

About Oaks Roofing & Construction

Our journey started in roofing sales, and over the years, we built a business focused on roofing and siding solutions that homeowners can count on. What began as a single trade skill evolved into a full-service roofing company with the ability to offer both repairs and replacements at competitive rates. Unlike larger companies with heavy overhead, we’re able to pass savings on to our customers and provide fair, transparent pricing.

Based in Goshen, OH, we take pride in serving local homeowners with a level of care and attention others often overlook. Many competitors skip repairs, pushing replacements instead. We believe in fixing what can be fixed. That mindset, paired with our multiple crews and hands-on service, gives our customers reliable results every time.

We handle everything from full roof replacements and storm damage repairs to siding installation and gutter replacements. Whether you need a quick repair or a full exterior overhaul, we’re ready to help. Our team brings value, experience, and craftsmanship to every project.

As a local roofing company serving the Goshen community, our mission is to protect your home and improve its exterior without unnecessary upsells or inflated prices. That’s what sets us apart. Let us show you how our approach makes a difference.
