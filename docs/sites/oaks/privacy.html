Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Oaks Roofing & Construction is a small roofing, siding, and gutter company in Goshen, OH. This website is a brochure: it tells you what we do and gives you a way to reach us. It is not a store, there are no accounts to create, and there is nothing to log in to. This page explains, in plain language, the only information this site handles.

What we collect

The only information we collect is what you choose to type into the quote form. That is:

  • Your first and last name
  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your ZIP code
  • The service you picked from the list
  • Whatever you write in the message box

That is the whole list. The form also carries one hidden field that is there to catch spam robots — if you are a person it stays empty, and its contents are thrown away either way. Calling us instead collects nothing on this website at all.

How we use it

We use what you send for one purpose: to answer your request and to quote the work. In practice that means calling or emailing you back, and often coming out to look at the roof, siding, or gutters in person. If you hire us, your contact details become part of the ordinary paperwork for your job.

We do not sign you up for a newsletter, and we do not send you marketing you did not ask for.

What we do not do

We do not sell, rent, or trade your information. We do not pass it to lead brokers, marketing companies, or anyone else looking to sell you something.

This site carries no advertising trackers, no ad pixels, and no analytics service. Nothing on these pages follows you around the web or reports back on where you go next.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies — none for tracking, none for advertising, none for analytics.

It does use one small flag in your browser's session storage, so that when you close the orange announcement bar at the top of the page it stays closed as you move from page to page. That flag lives on your device, clears itself when you close the tab, holds nothing but the fact that you dismissed the bar, and is never sent to us.

Third parties

Anything you send through the quote form goes into Oaks Roofing & Construction’s own customer records, where we keep track of quote requests and jobs, and we get a text or email telling us it arrived. It is not sent to a lead-generation service or a marketing platform, and we do not hand it on to anyone. If that ever changes, this page will say so before it does.

When you press Send Message, your browser also runs a quick automated check that you are a person and not a spam robot. That check looks at your browser, not at what you typed, and it is used only to keep junk out of our records. If the form cannot reach us, it opens a new email in your own mail app instead, addressed to us with your request filled in, and nothing is sent that way until you press send in your mail app.

Apart from that check, the one outside request these pages make is for the typefaces they are set in, which load from Google Fonts. Your browser asks Google's servers for those font files the same way it asks any web server for a file. We receive nothing from that request and do not use it to identify you.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete what you sent. Call (513) 827-5297, tell us you would like your quote request removed, and we will delete it. You do not need to give a reason, and it does not cost anything.

You can also just not use the form. The phone works every bit as well, and nothing on this site requires you to fill anything in to read it.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or about information you have already sent us? Call (513) 827-5297. We are based in Goshen, OH, and we answer Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and Sunday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.

Changes to this policy

If we change how this site handles your information, we will update this page and change the date at the top. We are not going to bury a change somewhere else.

Plain English Beats Fine Print

If anything here is unclear, or you want us to remove something you already sent, pick up the phone. We would rather answer the question than have you wonder.
