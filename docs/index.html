Straight Answers

Questions Homeowners Actually Ask

How much does a new roof cost in the Cincinnati area?

It depends on your roof's size, pitch, and the shingle you pick — but to give you a real number: most architectural-shingle replacements in Greater Cincinnati run about $740–$825 per roofing square installed, with budget and premium tiers landing roughly $615–$965 per square. Our free Instant Estimate gives you a tailored ballpark in about 60 seconds — no phone call, no pressure.

Do you do storm damage insurance jobs?

Absolutely — storm work is a big part of what we do. The claim is yours to file and decide. We document the damage properly, write a line-item estimate, and meet your adjuster on the roof after you file, so you're not stuck translating roofing jargon. I estimate in Xactimate — the same software your insurer prices the claim in — so I can read their scope line by line.

Do you offer financing?

Yes — through Acorn Finance, a lending marketplace that lets you check your options with a soft credit pull that doesn't affect your credit score. You'll see the actual lender, rate, and full terms before you commit to anything. If financing isn't the right move for your project, Joe will tell you straight.

What areas do you serve?

We cover Greater Cincinnati — across Southwest Ohio and into Kentucky. Cincinnati, Milford, Loveland, Mason, Batavia, West Chester and the surrounding communities are all areas I work regularly, and as of 2026 I also serve the Lexington area — Lexington, Georgetown, Nicholasville, Winchester, Richmond, and Versailles — on scheduled trips. Not sure if you're in range? See every area I serve or just call Joe and ask.

Are you insured and certified?

Yes — No Big Deal is fully insured, and we're factory-certified by the biggest names in roofing: GAF (GAF Certified) and TAMKO (Pro Gold). Those certifications unlock the strongest manufacturer warranties available — and you can verify each one directly with the manufacturer. On siding, I'm a member of the James Hardie Alliance. And as of 2026, No Big Deal is certified Locally Owned & Operated by American Operator — one local owner, not a franchise.
