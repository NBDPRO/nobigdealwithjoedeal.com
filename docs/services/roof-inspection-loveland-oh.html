Common questions

Roof Inspection FAQ — Loveland

Is a free roof inspection worth it for my Loveland home? ▸
Yes — specifically because I go on the roof. A drive-by or satellite assessment misses flashing failures, lifted ridge cap, and penetration damage that's invisible from ground level. You get photo documentation of every finding, a written summary, and an honest recommendation. If your roof is fine, I tell you. If it needs attention, you know exactly what and why.
How long does a roof inspection take? ▸
Most inspections take 45–90 minutes depending on roof size, complexity (number of penetrations, valleys, pitches), and findings. I also check gutters and fascia as part of the same visit. You get the photo report the same day — not a call-back with a summary two days later.
When should I get my Loveland roof inspected? ▸
After any significant hail or wind event is the most important trigger — hail damage isn't always visible from the ground, and missing it means missing the insurance claim window (typically 1–2 years from the event). Beyond storms, every 3–5 years is a reasonable maintenance cadence for roofs in the Clermont, Hamilton, and Warren County area, or any time you notice ceiling stains, granules in the gutters, or lifted shingles.
What does the inspection report include? ▸
I provide a written summary covering: overall shingle condition and estimated remaining life, specific areas of damage or concern with photos, flashing condition at all chimneys/vents/skylights, gutters and fascia, attic ventilation assessment if access is available, and a clear recommendation — repair, replace, or check back in X years. No padding, no manufactured urgency.
