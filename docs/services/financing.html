Because it's what today's cash can reach. When a repair genuinely fixes it, I'll quote you the repair and shake your hand. But on a roof that's at the end of its life, patch money is rent, not equity. It buys months. It never counts toward the roof you'll still have to buy.

FAQ

Financing Questions.
Real Answers.

Will applying affect my credit score?

Checking your options through Acorn Finance uses a soft credit pull, which does not affect your credit score. A hard inquiry only happens if you choose a specific lender's offer and complete the full application with that lender — and you'll know exactly when that step is.

What credit score do I need?

Acorn's lender network works with a wide range of credit profiles — that's the point of a marketplace. What you're offered depends on your credit, income, and the lender. Since checking is free and doesn't touch your score, the honest answer is: check, then decide. If financing isn't the right fit, Joe will tell you straight instead of pushing you into something that doesn't make sense.

Can I finance insurance work too?

Yes — financing can cover your deductible or any out-of-pocket portion of an insurance-covered project. This is one of the most common uses.

Is there a minimum project size for financing?

Generally financing makes the most sense for projects $3,000 and above. Ask Joe about your specific project and he'll give you a straight answer on whether it makes financial sense to finance.

Wouldn't a cheap repair now be smarter than financing a replacement?

Sometimes — and when it is, that's exactly what I'll quote you. If a repair genuinely solves it, a repair is the right call and there's nothing to finance. The trap is patching a roof that's already at the end of its life: those checks buy you months, not a roof, and none of that money counts toward the replacement you'll still need. When the roof is done, financing lets the project match what the house needs instead of what this month's cash allows. Check your real options with a soft pull and decide with actual numbers.

Who is the actual lender?

Acorn Finance is a lending marketplace, not a lender. Your loan comes from one of the vetted lending partners in their network — and you'll see that lender's name, rate, and full terms before you commit to anything.
