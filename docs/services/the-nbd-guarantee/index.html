The NBD Guarantee

Three roofs.
One promise.

The NBD Guarantee is three roof tiers: Standard (GAF Timberline NS), Preferred (Timberline HDZ), and Elite (Timberline UHDZ plus The NBD Build). On a typical home, Preferred runs roughly 20% over Standard and Elite roughly 40% over. Every tier comes with The Pledge: call me anytime, I come look, free.

Real Talk From Joe

Three tiers because three roofs deserve three answers.

I get it — three tiers can feel like a sales gimmick. So here's the actual truth: I built three tiers because three different homeowners walked into my life with three different roofs, and the same answer didn't fit all three.

The retired couple selling in two years to downsize doesn't need Elite. It would be wasted money. I'd put Standard on that roof and sleep fine. The young family in their forever home with a baby coming? That's where I push Preferred — better warranty, transferable to the eventual buyer, premium pipe boots that won't leak in year 7. The third group — the ones who tell me "this is the last roof I'm ever buying on this house" — that's Elite. Visible LumaNail fasteners so I can prove I did it right, me reaching out every year on the calendar to walk the roof and send them a report, my name on a certificate. They want to be done thinking about it.

None of this is upsell. The tier you should pick is the one that actually fits your situation. If you're getting pressure to go higher than what your honest answer says, that's not me — that's a contractor trying to hit a number.

Common Questions.

Why three tiers instead of one quote?

Because not every roof should be the same. A rental that's getting sold in 5 years doesn't need the Elite tier. A forever home does. Three tiers means the right roof for your situation, not the most expensive one I can sell you.

Can I mix and match between tiers?

Some of it, yes. The shingle grade is the spine of each tier — that doesn't mix. But individual upgrades like LumaNail or upgraded pipe boots can be added to a lower tier as a la carte if it's important to you. Ask Joe at the inspection.

What GAF warranty coverage do I get?

Joe is a GAF® Certified Contractor, which makes enhanced manufacturer warranty coverage (including System Plus) available on qualifying full-system installs. The warranty registered at install time is what applies — once it's on the paperwork, that's the contract. The NBD Lifetime Pledge — Joe coming back personally — is independent of GAF and applies for as long as Joe is in business, regardless.

Why isn't there a "different shingle brand" tier (Owens Corning, Atlas, CertainTeed)?

Honest answer: I install GAF because GAF's warranty system, install training, and accessory product line are the most coherent and best-supported in this market. I'm not going to pretend I'm equally fluent in every brand just to give you a brand-choice illusion. If you specifically want Owens Corning or another brand, talk to me — I'll tell you straight whether I think that's a good call for your roof.

Can I add LumaNail to a Standard or Preferred roof?

LumaNail is bundled into Elite. If you want it on a Standard or Preferred build, ask me and I'll work it into the bundle as a make-or-break upgrade — but I don't lead with it as an upcharge. Read about LumaNail → GAF Timberline Lineup

Why don't you publish exact tier prices?

Because every roof is different — pitch, square count, decking condition, accessibility, tear-off complexity, insurance documentation. Relative pricing is honest: Preferred is roughly 20% over Standard, Elite is roughly 40% over Standard. Exact numbers come from a free in-person estimate where I actually look at your roof.

What's the proactive annual outreach on Elite — how does that actually work?

It's written into the Elite contract. The reminder is on my calendar, not yours. Every year I call you, schedule a 20-minute roof check, get up there, photograph anything notable, and send you a one-page written report. No charge, no upsell, for the life of the roof. That's the difference between Elite and the lower tiers: every NBD roof has The Pledge (you can always call me), but only Elite has me reaching out to you on a schedule.
