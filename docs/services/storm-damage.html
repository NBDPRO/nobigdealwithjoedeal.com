Joe Deal has worked alongside insurance adjusters on hundreds of storm damage claims in Greater Cincinnati. Questions & Answers

Storm Damage & Insurance FAQ

Do I need to file a claim before calling you?
No — call Joe first. He'll inspect the damage and tell you honestly whether you have a claim worth filing. Filing unnecessarily can affect your rates, so it's worth knowing what you're working with before you contact your insurer.
How soon after a storm should I get my roof inspected?
As soon as possible. Insurance policies have claim filing deadlines, and documentation is always stronger while the damage is fresh. If you had a major storm in your area, call Joe and get on the schedule.
What does your storm inspection cover?
I inspect the full exterior — roof field, ridgeline, flashing, gutters, downspouts, siding, windows, AC unit top, and any other impact-susceptible surfaces. I'm looking for hail strikes, wind damage, and impact marks that adjusters need to see.
What if the insurance adjuster's estimate is lower than your repair estimate?
This happens. I can supplement the claim — which means providing documentation and line-item support for damage that was missed or undervalued in the adjuster's estimate. The insurance company isn't always wrong, but they're also not always thorough.
What insurance companies do you work with?
I work with all major carriers operating in Ohio — State Farm, Nationwide, Grange, Erie, Allstate, Progressive, and others. The process is similar regardless of carrier.
Is storm damage restoration covered by my deductible?
Your deductible applies to any insurance claim, regardless of the repair type — and it's yours to pay. No contractor can legally waive, absorb, or cover your deductible for you; anyone who offers to is committing insurance fraud that can put your claim at risk. Joe does it the honest way — he documents every bit of damage and fights for the full, legitimate payout, so your out-of-pocket stays as low as it honestly can.
