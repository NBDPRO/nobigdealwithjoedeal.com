Why an Impact-Rated Shingle Is Worth It in Hail Country.

Ohio and Kentucky take real hail. A standard architectural shingle bruises and cracks under repeated strikes — and once the mat fractures, the leak clock starts. Impact-rated shingles are tested under UL 2218: a 2-inch steel ball dropped from 20 feet without cracking the shingle's surface. Class 4 is the top of that scale.

  • Fewer claims, fewer headaches. A roof that shrugs off the hail you'd otherwise be filing on.
  • Possible insurance discount. Many OH/KY carriers discount the wind/hail portion of your premium for a documented Class 4 roof. See how that works ↓
  • HailGuard goes further still — the first asphalt shingle to back impact with a written hail warranty, available on every HailGuard roof I install.

Class 4 + Your Insurance.

Class 4 is the highest impact rating an asphalt shingle can earn — UL 2218 — and many homeowner-insurance carriers in Ohio and Kentucky offer a premium discount on the wind/hail portion of your policy when Class 4 shingles are installed and documented. StormFighter Flex and HailGuard both carry the Class 4 rating. The exact discount varies by carrier and ZIP code, but homeowners commonly save anywhere from $60 to $300+ per year on premium after moving to a Class 4 roof. For the real-world math on a Class 4 upsell — including a reader's $9,000 Duration-vs-HailGuard quote — I ran the numbers here, and if you're weighing StormFighter Flex against GAF's HDZ, a reader asked that too. A Lexington reader then put a real carrier quote on the same question — 12% off his premium for a Class 4 roof — and when he tried to buy HailGuard himself, he found out why it only comes through a contractor.

It's not automatic — you have to (1) ask your carrier whether they offer it, (2) get the savings figure in writing, and (3) submit your install documentation after the roof is on. I provide the product and installation paperwork as part of every Class 4 install, no extra cost.

1. Ask Your Carrier

Call your insurance agent before you sign. Ask: "Do you offer a discount on the wind/hail portion of my premium for installing UL 2218 Class 4 impact-resistant shingles?" Get the dollar amount in writing.

2. We Install + Document

I install your StormFighter Flex or HailGuard roof with the proper TAMKO system, then provide the product data and installation documentation showing the UL 2218 Class 4 rating. That's what your carrier needs.

3. You Submit + Save

Submit the documentation to your carrier. The discount typically applies on your next renewal. Many homeowners pay back the upgrade over a few years on the discount alone — and the roof outlasts the math by decades.

Quick Answers

What's the difference between StormFighter Flex and HailGuard?

Both are UL 2218 Class 4 impact shingles. StormFighter Flex delivers full Class 4 with a 160 mph WindGuard rating and a cold-weather install window; HailGuard sits at the top of the lineup, is tested beyond Class 4, and is the first asphalt shingle to carry a written hail warranty.

What does the 160 mph TAMKO wind rating require?

The full TAMKO system — TAMKO starter and TAMKO hip and ridge installed with the shingle. Without the system accessories, the headline wind rating doesn't apply.

What hail warranty comes with HailGuard?

Every correctly installed HailGuard roof carries the base HailGuard Limited System Warranty: a 10-year non-prorated Full Start period plus a 7-year hail warranty. Through a TAMKO Pro Gold certified contractor, qualifying systems can register the enhanced ProShield HailGuard warranty: a 20-year non-prorated Full Start plus a 10-year hail warranty.

Do TAMKO Class 4 shingles qualify for an insurance discount?

Often, yes — many carriers discount the wind/hail portion of the premium for documented Class 4 installs, commonly $60 to $300+ per year. Confirm your carrier's discount with your agent in writing before you commit.
