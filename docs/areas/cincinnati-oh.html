Local storm intel

Storm Damage in Cincinnati

⛈️ Why Cincinnati roofs are at risk

Cincinnati sits in one of Ohio's most active storm corridors. The Ohio Valley's warm, moist air collides with cold fronts rolling off the Great Lakes, producing the kind of severe thunderstorms that shred aging shingles.

Think your Cincinnati roof might have storm damage? I offer free roof inspections with photo documentation. If there's damage, I'll walk you through the insurance claim process step by step. If there isn't, I'll tell you honestly — no manufactured urgency.
