⛈️ Why West Chester roofs are at risk

West Chester's NOAA record since 2019 includes 2-inch hail near Kyles on May 16, 2025 and an EF0 tornado that started at Mason on March 30, 2025. With subdivisions of similar age spread across the township, one storm can put a lot of same-age roofs in play at once.

Local roof notes

The West Chester Roof Report

West Chester's housing has a median build year of 1988 (Census ACS), which makes the typical house about 38 years old. That puts a lot of these roofs on their second round. On houses this age the first thing I check is whether the last re-roof was a tear-off or an overlay, and how the OSB has held up at the eaves. Your house can be the exception, which is what an inspection is for.

⛈️ West Chester storm history, 2019–2026

32 severe-weather reports landed within about 6 miles of West Chester from 2019 through June 2026, per NOAA’s Storm Events Database. The standouts:

  • May 16, 2025 · 2-inch (hen-egg-size) hail at Kyles · NOAA record
  • March 30, 2025 · EF0 tornado, 9.73-mile path from Mason to Stubbs Mills (Warren Co.); NOAA damage estimate $215,000 · NOAA record
  • June 18, 2021 · 1-inch (quarter-size) hail at West Chester · NOAA record
  • April 8, 2020 · 1.25-inch (half-dollar-size) hail at Bethany · NOAA record

Hail of an inch or more was reported on 6 separate days in that span; the largest measured 2 inches.

Not sure the last storm did anything? I’ll get on the roof, photograph it, and give you a straight yes or no, free.

📋 Yes, a re-roof needs a permit

Butler County requires a Residential Roofing Application with a Re-Roofing Addendum: scope, roof plan, replaced decking and ventilation. West Chester Township's own office handles zoning. See: Butler County Building & Zoning.

Local knowledge

The West Chester Roofs I Actually See

West Chester is one of the biggest townships in Ohio, and the roofs tell you which decade you're standing in:

Beckett Ridge — the golf-course streets went up in the 80s and 90s, which puts many of those homes on their second roof, not their first. Second roofs are where I check decking and ventilation carefully: a re-roof done cheap twenty years ago is usually hiding something.

Wetherington — larger custom homes, complex rooflines, and an HOA with a real approval process. I do the color submittal and paperwork as part of the job, and the crew treats the property like the neighborhood expects.

The Tylersville and Union Centre corridors — wave after wave of 90s and 2000s subdivisions, most with builder-grade shingles installed street-by-street in the same season. When one house on the street gets approved for hail damage, it's worth having yours looked at — same storm, same shingles, same age.

Olde West Chester — the historic core along Cincinnati-Dayton Road carries older, steeper roofs where the right answer is sometimes a repair, not a replacement. I'll tell you which one you actually need.

Wherever you are in the township, I walk the roof personally and put what I find in writing.

Local questions

Asked in West Chester

Do I need a permit to replace my roof in West Chester?

Yes. Butler County requires a Residential Roofing Application with a Re-Roofing Addendum: scope, roof plan, replaced decking and ventilation. West Chester Township's own office handles zoning. Source: Butler County Building & Zoning. Rules change, so confirm for your address before the tear-off.

Has there been large hail near West Chester?

Yes. NOAA logged 8 reports of hail an inch or larger within about 6 miles of West Chester from 2019 through June 2026, on 6 days. The largest measured 2 inches (hen-egg size), on May 16, 2025 near Kyles. Storm cells are narrow, so a report down the road says nothing certain about your street.

How old are the houses in West Chester?

The median West Chester home was built in 1988, per the Census Bureau’s 2020–2024 survey, so half the houses are more than 38 years old. A lot of those roofs are the second the house has had, usually on OSB, and some went straight over the first layer. If you know when the last tear-off happened, that date matters more than the build year.

Do you cover all of West Chester?

All of it. Most of West Chester’s land falls in ZIPs 45069, 45241, 45246 and 45011, and I work every street. Butler County is part of my regular Greater Cincinnati territory. Every inspection is free, and I do it myself, on the roof, with photos.
