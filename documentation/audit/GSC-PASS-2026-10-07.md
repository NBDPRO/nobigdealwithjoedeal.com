## 3. Cannibalization and the #2251 split

- **"roof replacement cincinnati" is won by a blog post**, not the Cincinnati
  town page. `/blog/how-long-does-roof-replacement-take-cincinnati` carries it
  at ~10; `/services/roof-replacement-cincinnati-oh` drew 2 impressions and
  `/areas/cincinnati-oh` sits at 34.7. The post's body link pointed at the
  region hub. Fixed: the "roof replacement in the Cincinnati area" link now goes
  to the Cincinnati town page (#2251's rule: town page = "Cincinnati, OH").
- **"free roof inspection"** queries still land on the `/services/roof-inspection`
  hub (101 of the "roof inspection" impressions); `/inspect` drew 5. #2251 moved
  the anchors and hub title today, so this is expected to shift; no further edit.
- **StormFighter Flex queries split across two posts.** The storm-series post
  takes most "stormfighter flex" impressions (positions 15–20) while the
  dedicated HDZ-vs-Flex post converts better (3 clicks / 94, 3.2%). The Flex
  post's title now says "Review" (the queries say "reviews", "warranty").
  Folding the two posts is the 10-30 decision.
- **West Chester / Mason James Hardie queries land on the Loveland siding page**
  (31 + 23 impressions), which means `/services/siding-replacement-west-chester-oh`
  and `-mason-oh` are weak or not indexed (`siding-replacement-mason-oh` is
  "Discovered – not indexed", see §4).
- **Brand query** shows town pages (Loveland 24, Indian Hill 17, Cincinnati 17
  impressions) beside the homepage. Sitelink behaviour, not a problem.
- The hub/town split itself is not visible yet: four hubs (`/services/roof-repair`,
  `/siding-replacement`, `/gutter-replacement`, `/siding-repair`) have zero
  impressions because they are not indexed at all (§4). The hubs that are
  indexed: `/services/roof-inspection` (124), `/services/wood-siding-repair`
  (27, position 5.2), `/services/roof-replacement` (6).
