## Fixes
- **"Generated by NBD Pro" on customer paper** — the SaaS product's name: the generic renderer (Permit
  Application has no renderer of its own), the finalized estimate, the classic estimate export, the scope-from-
  drawing and supplement sheets, and the body of the email sent to the homeowner (whose subject also fell back to
  "NBD Document"). Removed for every tenant.
- **Classic estimate export** hard-coded NBD's wordmark, title and footer ("No Big Deal Home Solutions — Greater
  Cincinnati"). NBD renders exactly as before; another tenant gets its own legal name.
- **Pre-flight prefill** filled NBD's company name / phone / email, "NBD Inspector" and "Joe Deal" on every
  tenant's documents. NBD keeps them; another tenant gets its own values, "<name> Inspector", or a blank for the rep.
  Keyed on the platform-tenant check (companyId), never on brand strings.
- **Retired colours hidden as RGB triplets** — a hex grep can't see them: the customer estimate PDF fell back to
  `[30, 58, 110]` (#1E3A6E) / `[232, 114, 12]` (#E8720C) beside a comment saying NBD's colours are #1A3057 /
  #BD5728; the invoice "mark paid" method picker tinted with `rgba(232,114,12,.10)`. Now the current navy/orange.
- **CSS comments shipped inside document `<style>` blocks** (internal notes as searchable customer-document text —
  the receipt "BALANCE DUE" failure class). `getHTML` strips them; `printCSS()` already did for its own.
- **company-profile.js** colour comment said the LIVE palette was "retired" (a past hex sweep rewrote the list).
