**Method:** a Playwright script opened each view with `goTo()`. On each one it recorded:
- horizontal page scroll, and elements wider than the screen outside a scrolling box
- controls under the target size: 24 px (WCAG 2.5.8 AA) everywhere, and 44 px (the house rule for phones, DESIGN.md)
- buttons with no accessible name, and inputs with no label

Screenshots were looked at by eye for the visual findings. The script and the screenshots stay in the session scratchpad, not the repo.

**Not covered yet:** the marketing site, `customer.html`, the portal, e-sign, the Success Tracker, and dark themes.
Those are pass 2.

- Every view rendered content. The four AI tool views were empty only because #2726 is not merged yet.

inconsistently, and the placeholder disappears once someone types:

| Photos | `photoSearchInput`, `photoLeadSelect` |

| Reports | `reportRangeStart`, `reportRangeEnd`, `reportCompareMode` |
| Ask Joe | `joeInput` |

Money has one icon-only button with no name (`.mdx-btn-wrap`).

### C. Phone targets between 24 and 44 px (Low, design call)

Many phone controls are 30–40 px tall: tab rows (Storm, Academy, Settings), filter chips (Products,
Job Templates, Talk Tank), the "＋ NEW …" buttons and Pipeline's search and type filter. They pass AA but not
the house 44 px phone rule. Raising them all changes how dense every view looks, so this is **Jo's call**.

### D. Visual consistency (Low–Medium, by eye)

- **Product Library header buttons:** the four buttons are four colours: orange "+ Add Product", white
  "Price Book", **green "Export CSV"** and **red "Reset"**. Green and red are not brand button colours
  (DESIGN.md: orange primary, quiet secondary). A red primary-weight "Reset" also invites a mis-tap.
- **Storm Center (phone):** the stat cards row cuts off its third card at the screen edge, with no sign it scrolls.
- **Prospects (phone):** the Total / Hot / Warm / Cold tiles cut off "Cold" the same way.

### E. Sample account only (Low)

- **A raw function name** shows when a sample screen hits a server feature, e.g. `"callPipelineHealth" runs on NBD
  Pro servers…` and `"getAdjusterTacticBoard" runs on…`. The second one also names an insurance tool.
  The plain-words message should not name the function.
- **Money's Stripe card** prints "getStripeOverview" the same way.
- **Admin** logs a `listTeamMembers` console error.

## Pass 3 (later 2026-10-24): text contrast on the light themes, and the Success Tracker

**Success Tracker:** clean. The 2026-10-23 a11y pass holds: no undersized control, no unlabeled input, no sideways scroll at 390 or 1280 px.

**Text contrast.** A script walked every text node on eight CRM views (Home, Pipeline, Schedule, Prospects, Estimates, Money,
Reports, Settings), computed its effective background through the stacked surfaces, and flagged anything under
WCAG AA (4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text). On the default light theme it found ~60 failing text styles. Two causes:

1. **Status colours used as small text.** Green `#16a34a` (3.1:1), gold `#d97706` (3.0:1), blue `#3b82f6` (3.5:1), and a
   dozen hard-coded Tailwind hexes written for the dark theme (`#f59e0b` 1.9:1, `#fcd34d` 1.2:1, `#eab308` 1.7:1 …),
   on chips, trend arrows, tile values and column headers.
2. **Brand orange as text.** `#BD5728` on the page background is 4.37:1, a hair under 4.5. `.panel-label` alone put it on
   every panel in the CRM.

**Fix:** the pattern `DESIGN.md` already set for accent and danger text, extended. Three new tokens in `ui-primitives.css`,
`--ui-success-text`, `--ui-warn-text`, `--ui-info-text` (the status colour pulled halfway to `--t`), and the flagged rules
read them: `.panel-label`, the pipeline chips (`.kct-*`, `.kc-days-*`, `.kc-task-badge`, the score and job-type chips),
`.btn-red`, the Prospects tiles and status labels, the Money tiles and overdue badges, the Reports trend arrows and ROI
totals, the Schedule links, and the engagement tiers. Fills, borders and dots keep the plain colour, so nothing changes
hue; text gets darker on light themes and lighter on dark ones. Two kanban column headers at 4.45:1 (`#a16207`) moved
to `#854d0e`. **Result: all eight views clean on the default theme.** The test computes each token's contrast on the
`nbd-original`, `paper` and `presentation` surfaces (`ui-sweep-a11y-2026-10-24`, pass 3).

**Left as is, for Jo:**
- **Midnight theme accent.** Its buttons are white on `#6366f1`, 4.47:1 on every view. A theme palette choice; a
  slightly deeper indigo (`#5b5fe0`) would clear 4.5.
- The `lsroi` win-rate total in Reports (22 px bold, 2.98:1 against 3:1) now reads the success token and passes.

**Not covered:** e-sign (`/pro/sign`, `/pro/esign`) needs a live signing token, so it is outside what the sample
account can drive. The 14 other CRM themes were not swept for text contrast; the tokens apply to all of them.

## Pass 4 (later 2026-10-24): the other 14 CRM views, and a dark theme in light mode

Same contrast script on D2D, Storm, Photos, Templates, Calls, Messaging, Expenses, Products, Job Templates, Signs,
Draw, Visits, Training and Academy (default theme), and on the Obsidian theme (a dark theme, as a light-OS phone shows it).

**Real breakages found and fixed:**
- **Obsidian's buttons were white on grey (3.4:1) on every view.** The theme engine kept white ink on any accent over
  3:1. It now picks whichever of white or dark ink reads better on the final accent; brand orange and every accent
  white already suited keep white.
- **Draw's "Shortcuts" hint was dark text on a dark card** on light themes (`color: var(--t)` on a fixed dark card).
- **Academy's tab bar and labels** (a surface that stays dark on every theme) used the dark-theme accent `#A14A22`
  (3.1:1) and a grey `#6B7280` (3.5:1). They use the light brand orange and a lighter grey now; fills keep `#BD5728`.

**Same token moves as pass 3,** on the Storm chips, D2D colour utilities (`.dk-c-*`), Photos labels, the Templates
library (21 category and badge colours), Products (category titles, unit chips, tier labels, margin colours, the
tier filters), Job Templates (category chips, pills, bands), Draw (the mode buttons, labels, the four coloured ghost
buttons), Training (difficulty and NEW labels) and the `.fg-orange` text utility. Two filled buttons under white text
moved to darker fills (Products' Edit `#3b82f6` → `#1d4ed8`; Draw's Save and go buttons `var(--green)` → `#15803d`).

**Result:** every view clean on the default theme except icon-only glyphs and map labels (false positives: emoji
icons, and labels drawn over map tiles). Obsidian: Home, Money and Settings clean.

**Left as is, for Jo:**
- Obsidian's muted text on a tinted card (`.fa-addr`, 4.27:1): that theme's `--m`.
- The Training "Objection Obliterator" banner is a fixed navy gradient with its own light text; its green START is
  4.2:1 on the navy. A banner palette choice.
