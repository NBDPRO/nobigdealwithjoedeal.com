# IKO ROOFPRO Advantage — program facts (read from IKO's own pages)

Date: 2026-10-13. Public sources only; no logins, no forms, no account data.
No contractor ID or account number appears in this note by design.

## Bottom line for public copy

1. **An Advantage-tier member may not hold themselves out to the public as a
   ROOFPRO member.** IKO's own footnote: Advantage members "shall not
   represent themselves to the public as members of the ROOFPRO Program,
   including use of any ROOFPRO-branded marketing materials, literature and
   merchandise until they have met the requirements of, and advanced to, the
   Select or Craftsman Premier level." So no ROOFPRO / ROOFPRO Advantage badge,
   "IKO ROOFPRO contractor" line or "ROOFPRO Advantage" claim on
   nobigdealwithjoedeal.com, GBP, trucks, yard signs or estimates until Select.
2. **No extended / ROOFPRO warranty at Advantage.** Extended Iron Clad
   Protection requires the roof be installed by a ROOFPRO Select or Craftsman
   Premier contractor. Advantage gets only IKO's standard shingle limited
   warranty (which every homeowner gets with IKO shingles regardless of
   contractor).
3. **The $1.50/sq rebate is real** but is a purchase rebate to Jo, not a
   homeowner benefit, and has a 500-square/year floor in the U.S.
4. Jo's badge reads ROOFPRO / IKO / ADVANTAGE. IKO's benefits page describes
   the Advantage logo as an "IKO Advantage shield logo with the red knight
   emblem and black IKO wordmark" (no ROOFPRO word) and the Select/Craftsman
   badges as "ROOFPRO Select / Craftsman Premier badge". The 2025 Advantage
   flyer headline does say "SIGN UP FOR ROOFPRO ADVANTAGE". Treat the badge as
   an internal/supplier-facing credential, not a public mark.

## Notes on Jo's earlier note ("$1.50/sq, 500 sq/yr of architectural or better")

Confirmed in substance: $1.50/sq, 500 sq/yr minimum in the U.S., signed
agreement required. Correction: the minimum counts "Architectural, Performance
or Designer" shingles, but the rebate is paid only on the named Performance /
Designer lines (Dynasty family, Nordic, Armourshake, Royal Estate, Crowne
Slate) in the 2025 flyer. Do not assume Cambridge earns it.

## What NBD copy may and may not say (until Select)

- OK, factual: "We install IKO shingles." Pair with the standard IKO limited
  warranty as written by IKO. Existing site rule stands: GAF/TAMKO are the
  promoted systems; IKO is an additional supplier, so check page claims
  against `documentation/` brand notes before adding IKO.
- Not OK: "IKO ROOFPRO contractor", "ROOFPRO Advantage" badge or logo on any
  public surface, "IKO certified/preferred/authorized", "IKO-backed" or
  "extended Iron Clad warranty" offers. None of those are available to an
  Advantage member.
- Never imply IKO employs, endorses or warrants the contractor's workmanship.
- Internal use (CRM, supplier paperwork, rebate claims) of the badge is fine.
