## The repeatable "Reader Questions" pipeline (keep the flywheel turning)

Every out-of-area call or text with a real question =  a post within the week:
1. Jo forwards the question (screenshot or paraphrase) to a Claude session with
   "Reader Questions post: <the question>".
2. Session writes it publish-ready in the established format (the two 2026-08-17
   posts are the spec: sourced-claims-only, FAQPage schema, Reader Questions tag,
   dual local/national CTA), opens the PR, Jo verifies the claims table + merges.
3. Session pings IndexNow + requests GSC indexing for the new URL post-merge.
4. The question-asker gets the link — they asked, so they'll read it, share it,
   and sometimes link it. That's the most natural backlink loop a contractor can run.
