## Notes on the copy

- **No claim here that the site can't back.** Every description maps to a live
  `/services/` page. Two things were deliberately kept out: any quantified claim
  about other contractors (the *"most roofers won't schedule a shed"* form was
  cut from the site the same day — see
  [SHED-OUTBUILDING-GAP §4c](../audit/SHED-OUTBUILDING-GAP-2026-09-03.md)), and
  any price or warranty promise beyond what the Pledge actually says.
- **"Rot is usually not an insurance claim"** is deliberate in service 1. It
  costs claim work and it is the single most trust-building line on the new wood
  page — same move as the $140 second opinion.
- **Fascia and soffit has no page of its own yet.** It is the one service in the
  list selling something the site only covers inside another hub. If GBP starts
  showing impressions for it, that is the signal to build the page — logged as
  open in the shed note's §5.
