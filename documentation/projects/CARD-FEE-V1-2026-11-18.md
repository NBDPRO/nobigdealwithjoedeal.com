# Card fee v1 (2026-11-18): research, what shipped, what did not

Flag: `docCardFeeV1`, per person (`feature_flags/_default` or `feature_flags/{uid}`, only boolean `true` is on), **default OFF**, registered in `functions/flags-registry.js` `FIRESTORE_FLAGS`.

Jo's decisions (Q7, Q8, Q26, Q46 in [the deep sweep](TEMPLATE-DEEP-SWEEP-2026-11-17.md); clause PAY-7 in [the attorney packet](ATTORNEY-REVIEW-PACKET-2026-11-18.md)): 3% on **credit** cards only, never debit or prepaid, none on Kentucky insurance-paid jobs, capped at the processor's actual cost, its own line before the pay button and on the receipt, refunded proportionally, only for contracts signed after the fee was added, none on Zelle, check, ACH, wire or cash. Related: [clause set v1](CLAUSE-SET-V1-2026-11-18.md) (PAY-7 prints the card-fee paragraph only when `paymentTerms.cardFeePct > 0`).

## Verdict (short)

**The fee mechanism is NOT built and no fee is charged anywhere.** A credit-only surcharge is possible on Stripe, but only through a different integration than the one this repo uses (a Payment Intent confirmed from Stripe's Payment Element, with the card known before the amount is fixed). That is a new payment path, not a small change. Under no circumstance was a flat fee added to all cards. What shipped is the pure fee logic, the records, the disclosure text and the refund math, all dark and with `CHARGING_LIVE = false`, so the disclosure cannot print and nothing is charged.

## Step 0: what the repo does today (read from the code)

- **NBD platform tenant:** a real Stripe **Invoice** (`functions/stripe-crm-invoice.js`: `collection_method: 'send_invoice'`, `auto_advance: false`, finalized, then the hosted invoice URL is handed to the homeowner; it checks `fin.amount_due === balanceDueCents`). The homeowner pays on **Stripe's hosted invoice page**. Payment methods requested by `AchPay.createWithAch` in `functions/ach-payments.js`: card, Link, `us_bank_account`.
- **Connect tenants:** a single-use **Payment Link** (`stripe.paymentLinks.create` in `functions/stripe.js` ~1628) with a fixed `price_data` line (`unit_amount: balanceDueCents`), destination charge plus `application_fee_amount`.
- **Credit path:** the `payment_intent.succeeded` webhook (`functions/stripe.js` ~1998) credits **`amount_received`** to `amountPaid` and to a `payments[]` entry, keyed on the PaymentIntent id (idempotent), and decides "fully paid" when the balance reaches 0. So **any extra amount charged on the card would be credited as invoice payment**, i.e. a fee would silently shrink the homeowner's balance. A surcharge needs this math changed (credit `amount - fee`, store the fee separately); that is a money-path change, not a wiring detail.
- There is **no Stripe Payment Element, no client-created PaymentIntent and no own pay page**: the portal only links out to Stripe's hosted page (`functions/portal-pay.js` says "nothing in this file talks to Stripe"). `js.stripe.com` is allowed by the CSP, but no checkout code uses it.
- Refunds: `functions/payment-reversal.js` and `functions/connect-refund-logic.js` handle reversals; neither knows about a fee.
- The invoice pay panel (`docs/pro/js/document-generator-templates.js` ~1296) lists Check, Zelle, "Credit Card — ask for secure link" (only when there is no pay URL) and Financing. That line does not promise a fee and does not conflict, so it was **left alone**.

## Step 0: what Stripe's official docs say (fetched 2026-11-18)

Sources: [Collect surcharges (API)](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/cards/surcharge), [Collect surcharges (Checkout / Payment Element, automatic surcharge)](https://docs.stripe.com/payments/advanced/surcharge), and the Payment Element card-funding filter docs found by search.

1. **Surcharge API (public preview, API version `2026-03-25.preview`).** Set `amount_details[surcharge][amount]` on a **PaymentIntent** (create, update, confirm or capture). **You** calculate the amount; Stripe does not add it: "you need to specify the total amount inclusive of the surcharge in the `amount` field". With a payment method attached Stripe returns `surcharge.status` (`available` / `unavailable`, "based on the country and the funding type of the payment method") and `surcharge.maximum_amount`. Table: United States, **credit cards only**, **maximum 3%**. So the funding check (credit vs debit and prepaid) is **funding-aware on the PaymentIntent**, after the payment method is known.
2. **Automatic surcharge on Checkout (private preview, request access).** Calculates the surcharge from card brand, funding type and issuing country. US only, cards and Apple Pay, `payment` mode only, **Payment Element only** (`ui_mode=elements`), needs a third-party surcharge provider app (Yeeld or InterPayments) installed from the Stripe App Marketplace.
3. **Payment Element can reject card funding types** (`disallowedCardFundingTypes`). Stripe says this is browser-side validation; enforce on the server by reading `payment_method_preview.card.funding` from the ConfirmationToken before confirming.
4. **Stripe's compliance text** (both pages): comply with law and network rules; do not exceed the card networks' limits or your cost of acceptance; **notify your acquirer or card network** where required; surcharge consistently; **disclose conspicuously before purchase** and let the customer cancel or switch method after seeing it; show it **separately on the receipt**; return the **full surcharge on a full refund and a prorated surcharge on a partial refund** (their example: $60 of a $100 order with a $1.50 surcharge refunds $60.90). "You're fully responsible for any fines, penalties, or losses."
5. **Prebuilt Stripe receipts show the surcharge** automatically; `amount_details.surcharge.amount` on the PaymentIntent is the field to read.

### What this means for the products the repo uses

| Product | Credit-only surcharge feasible? |
| --- | --- |
| **Payment Link** (`price_data`, fixed `unit_amount`) | **No.** The amount is fixed when the link is minted, before any card is entered; the funding type is unknown then. A fixed fee on the link would be charged to debit and prepaid cards too, and to ACH on the same link. I found **no** Stripe doc that lets a Payment Link add a surcharge after the card is read. **Not verified:** whether Payment Links accept the preview surcharge fields; I found nothing saying they do. |
| **Hosted Stripe Invoice** (NBD) | **No, same reason** (the invoice total is finalized before payment; one amount for every method). **Not verified:** whether the hosted invoice page supports the surcharge preview; no doc found. |
| **Checkout Session** | Only via the **private-preview** automatic surcharge (Payment Element, `ui_mode=elements`, a provider app, access request). Not available to the repo today and not a drop-in replacement for the hosted invoice. |
| **PaymentIntent + Payment Element** | **Yes**, funding-aware: the server creates the intent, the client collects the card with the Payment Element, the server reads the funding, computes the fee with `computeCardFee`, and updates/confirms with `amount_details[surcharge]` (public preview API). This is the only fit, and it needs a new pay page. |

**Could not verify:** whether this Stripe account has the preview enabled; the preview's behaviour with Connect destination charges (Connect tenants) and with Apple Pay / Google Pay; the real per-card processing cost field to use for the "actual cost" cap (Stripe's `balance_transaction.fee` exists only after the charge, so a cap computed before confirming would use an estimate, which is a legal question, AQ12); whether ACH on the same intent is affected.

## Step 3 verdict: not implemented

The only funding-aware path is a new PaymentIntent and Payment Element pay page on a **preview** API, plus a change to the money-credit webhook, refund path and ledgers. That is not a small, safe change, and the account's preview access is unconfirmed, so **nothing was wired and no fee is charged.** `cardFeePct` must stay **0** (the Company Profile default). With it at 0 the contract paragraph does not print, and even if someone set it above 0, the new disclosure stays off because `CHARGING_LIVE` is false (an invoice must never promise a fee the system does not charge). Setting `cardFeePct` above 0 today would still print the PAY-7 card-fee paragraph on new contracts (clause set v1, flag `docClauseSetV1`) while no fee is charged: **do not set it.**

## Step 1: `computeCardFee` (shipped)

`functions/card-fee.js` and `docs/pro/js/card-fee.js` (byte-identical twins, pinned by the test; browser global `NBDCardFee`).

`computeCardFee({ amountCents, funding, cardFeePct, state, paymentSource, contractHasClause, processorCostCents? })` returns `{ feeCents, reason }`. The fee is zero unless every gate passes, checked in this order: `BAD_AMOUNT` (not a positive integer), `FEE_OFF` (`cardFeePct` missing, 0, negative or not a finite number), `NO_CLAUSE` (`contractHasClause !== true`), `KY_INSURANCE` (state Kentucky **or unreadable**, and source insurance **or unreadable**: the same fail-closed reading as clause set v1), `NOT_CREDIT` (funding is anything but exactly `credit`, so debit, prepaid and unknown are free), then `OK`. The percentage is capped at 3.0, the fee is rounded half-up to whole cents with integer arithmetic (basis points), and an optional `processorCostCents` caps the fee at the actual cost (negative or non-numeric cost is ignored as "not supplied", never as zero). It never returns a negative number.

`refundFeeCents(feeCents, paymentCents, refundCents)`: the pro-rata share, half-up, a full refund returns the whole fee, never more than the fee.

## Step 2: disclosure and records (shipped, dark)

- `disclosureLine(...)` returns "Pay free by Zelle, check, ACH or wire (and cash). Credit cards carry a {pct}% fee; debit and prepaid cards do not." only when the flag is on, `CHARGING_LIVE` is true, `cardFeePct > 0`, the contract has the paragraph and the job is not Kentucky insurance. Today it returns '' because `CHARGING_LIVE` is false.
- `paymentRecordFields(...)` returns `{ cardFeeCents, cardFeePct, fundingType }` for a payment record (zero fee and the funding as Stripe reported it, `unknown` when absent).
- The invoice pay panel prints the disclosure line (never today), and the invoice totals and the receipt print the fee as its own line **only** when the payment data carries `cardFeeCents > 0` and the flag is on. With the flag off, every document is byte-identical: goldens in `tests/fixtures/card-fee-v1-golden.json` were recorded from origin/main before any renderer changed (`node tests/card-fee-v1-2026-11-18.test.js --print`).
- The "Credit Card — ask for secure link" tile and the Payment Agreement "Credit Card" tile were not changed: neither states a fee.
