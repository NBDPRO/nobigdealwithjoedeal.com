## The flag

`docAtticAckV1`, per person (`feature_flags/_default` or `feature_flags/{uid}`, only boolean `true` is on), **default OFF**, in `functions/flags-registry.js` `FIRESTORE_FLAGS`. Off: every path behaves as before (the phase 2 golden hashes still pass). Turn on together with `docEvidenceFoundation` and `docClauseSetV1`; the server reads it strictly (a read error refuses). **Review 2026-11-18:** the gate is ON when the acting user's flag **or the tenant owner's flag** (the lead's `companyId`, an owner's own uid) is on, so a rep without the flag cannot sign a re-roof freely under an owner who has it. Both reads are strict (`atticGateOn` in `functions/doc-evidence.js`).

## Data model

- `leads/{id}.scope`: `reroof | repair | siding | gutters | other`. Default from the estimate (any row/line with code `LAB TO...` = reroof; `workKind` repair = repair; else other). **The stored scope is only a floor (review 2026-11-18):** the server derives the scope as the maximum of the stored scope, its own `atticScopeFloor` (see below) and the scope of each estimate in play (the one being sent / signed / minted, the lead's primary, and, when neither resolves, the lead's own estimates). A rep can raise it, never lower it; `firestore.rules` deny a lowering update and any write of `atticScopeFloor`. No pricing change.
- `leads/{id}.atticScopeFloor`: `'reroof'`, server-written (admin SDK) the first time the gate sees a re-roof, so taking the tear-off line off an estimate after a first send does not lower it. Client-unwritable.
- `leads/{id}.atticCheck` (rep-written): `status` inspected | not_inspected, `reason` (declined, scheduling_homeowner, scheduling_nbd, no_access, unsafe, no_attic, other), `who` (homeowner | nbd | conditions), `reasonText`, `notes`, `inspectedOn`, `accessPoint`, `conditions{key:true}`, `conditionOther`, `vent{...}`, `recs[{id,qty}]`, `photos[{id,kind}]` (photo ids in the CRM photo collection, tagged `attic`), `version`, `recordedBy`, `recordedAt`. Q69 minimums are enforced by `validate()`.
- Signed form: a `leads/{id}/documents` row of type `attic_acknowledgment` or `attic_not_inspected`. **`atticAckResult`** (server-owned in `firestore.rules`): `{version, notInspected, declined, manifestOk, complete, acknowledgments[{id,checked}], formDateMs, attests}`. `complete` is true only when the stored HTML **matches the server's own manifest** (below) and every required tick and Yes/No is set. `signatureEvidence.acknowledgments` carries the same ticks.
- `attic_overrides/{id}` (write:false in rules): `{leadId, companyId, byUid, byName, kind, reason, scope, toScope?, estimateId, estimateStamp, at, expiresAt, revokedAt?, revokedBy?, version}`, written only by `recordAtticOverride`. Kind `proceed` (job proceeds without a signed page) or `scope_lowered` (the only way a lead's scope goes down). Each is **bound to the estimate it covers** (id plus a digest of its priced content, so a re-priced estimate needs a new one), **expires 30 days** after it is recorded, and an owner can **revoke** it (`{ revoke: true }`). A record with no expiry (the old shape) does not count.

## Deviation: ticks instead of initials

The generated-HTML signing paths only support signature canvases. The homeowner's Yes/No per line and each acknowledgment are **required tick controls** (`data-nbd-ack`), recorded with the signature, not initials. The signature widget refuses to finish with a missing tick; the server re-checks (remote: from the original document plus the submitted tick states; in-person: from the stored signed copy) and records every tick. The printed form carries one neutral line: "Tick each box and each Yes or No. Your ticks are saved with your signature." Counsel should confirm ticks plus signature suffice; the packet says "initials".

## Q18 override

`recordAtticOverride` (callable): the tenant's owner (the uid is the tenant), the `company_admin` of the same company, or a platform admin only (a rep never, and a company member who created the lead is not its owner); reason 10 to 500 characters. A sent but unsigned form is notice, never signed; only a signed form with a server result, or an override, clears the gate.

## Security review fixes, 2026-11-18

An independent review of this branch found eight issues; each is fixed or documented here. Tests: `attic-ack-review-2026-11-18` (each item a section), plus the emulator rules file.

1. **Scope** can no longer be lowered (above). The estimate actually being sent is passed into the gate at the e-sign envelope, the deal mint, the remote link and in-person recording; with no estimate in play the lead's own estimates are read.
2. **Forged acknowledgment.** The server holds a manifest per document type (`manifestFor` / `verifyManifest` in `functions/attic-gate.js`): the exact required tick ids (six acknowledgments, plus four statements on the not-inspected page), the Yes/No line ids (the known ventilation lines and the two third-party lines, each with both a Yes and a No, none required), the variant marker `<!-- nbd:attic-ack ATTIC_ACK_V1 inspected|not_inspected -->` for that type, and the packet's own paragraphs. A stored copy with an extra, missing or non-required control, a stripped required flag, the wrong type's marker, a changed paragraph, or hand-written HTML is not complete. The remote link re-validates the rep-authored original before the submission is accepted (before the burn). The ids mirror the renderer and the real render is pinned against the manifest in the test.
3. **Flag per user**: either flag (above).
4. **Deal with no resolvable lead**: with the flag on, the scope comes from the deal's own estimate; a deal that resolves neither a lead nor an estimate is refused (fail closed). A re-roof deal with no lead can never be minted (no signed form can exist without a lead).
5. **In-person gate**: documented as not enforced (limits above); not made to look enforced.
6. **Override**: bound, 30 days, revocable, owner or admin of the tenant only.
7. **Free-check task**: one task per lead (`attic-check-<leadId>`, a set), due no earlier than tomorrow, and `atticFreeCheckDays` must be at least 1 (rules bound, `clause-set.js` bound, and the form treats 0 as unset).
8. This note.

## Second review fixes, 2026-11-18

A second review of the same branch found these; each has a test in `attic-ack-review2-2026-11-18` (and the rules file) and the flag stays default OFF; flag-off behaviour and the phase 2 golden hashes are unchanged.

1. **Deal mint.** `deal_rooms.leadId` and `estimateId` are written by the rep. Under the flag, a deal whose `leadId` resolves to no lead in the tenant is refused (it used to be dropped silently, leaving the gate to read only the rep's chosen estimate), and so is a deal whose estimate is neither the lead's primary nor carries that lead's id. A deal with no `leadId` at all keeps the phase 3 rule (gated from its own estimate, refused with neither).
2. **Size and cost.** `verifyManifest` refuses a copy over 2 MB (`too_large`, with a plain message on the remote link and in person). The tag, comment and `<script>`/`<style>` stripping is one linear pass (`_scan`); `parseAcks` and `applyAcks` find tag ends with `indexOf`. Timing tests use 1 MB of repeated `<!--`, `<script`, `<input` and `<` (the old regexes took about 20 s at 256 KB).
3. **Hidden wording.** The form fails the manifest on a `<template>`, a `hidden` attribute, any inline `style` other than a margin (and the signature image's own), hiding CSS in a style block (including print-only rules; the print button's one rule is the exception), the `no-print` class on wording, a stylesheet link, a control that is not a checkbox or radio input, or an unterminated comment/tag. The stored ORIGINAL must carry no `checked` on any control (`preticked`); a signed copy keeps its ticks (`verifyManifest(type, html, { signed: true })`). Text inside comments, `title`, `noscript`, `canvas`, `object` and `iframe` is not read as wording.
4. **Rows and authority.** `decide` ignores form rows with `deleted: true` or status `deleted`, and a signed form counts only for the estimate (id and stamp, the same binding an override has) it was signed against, written by the remote and in-person paths (`formBasis`). A re-priced or re-scoped estimate needs a new form. A legacy lead with no `companyId` has no company owner: only a platform admin can record or revoke an override on it.
5. **Free-check task** is created only if absent (a read first; an unreadable task is not overwritten), so a lost `freeCheckTaskCreated` flag cannot reopen a completed task.
6. **Rules tests**: a rep who owns the lead cannot lower scope (update, field/value, `FieldPath`, merge, `mergeFields`, or a set that drops it) and nobody can delete or change `atticScopeFloor`.
7. **In-person flag-off.** The attic flag reads in `recordInPersonSignature` are no longer strict: a read that fails reads "not known on", so an unreadable owner flag doc cannot refuse flag-off signing. The evidence-flag reads before it were already strict on main and are unchanged.
8. **CI budget.** `BUCKET_BUDGET_MS` stays 8 minutes: the manifest job's `timeout-minutes` is 10, and raising the budget to 11 minutes would need the job timeout at 14 or more. Not changed here.

Limits kept: the in-person signed copy is produced on the rep's device, so a pre-ticked box in it cannot be told from a tick; only the remote path's ORIGINAL is held to "no ticks". Estimate binding is empty-to-empty when a lead has several estimates and no primary.

## Third review fixes, 2026-11-18

Tests: `attic-ack-review3-2026-11-18`. The flag stays default OFF and flag-off behaviour is unchanged.

1. **The real form passes the manifest.** `generate()` injects `<style id="nbd-sig-styles">` (it hides the signing controls in print) and the page opens with a print rule that hides the print button; the old denylist rejected both, so every real form failed (`css_hidden`). The style blocks are now an allowlist: each must equal a known block (`AA.SIG_STYLES_CSS`, `AA.ATTIC_FORM_CSS`, in the twinned `attic-ack.js`, which the generator, template and library also read), or be the page block, which must open with exactly `AA.PRINT_RULE_CSS` and then hold only rules from a property allowlist (no hiding value). Any other `<style>` fails (`style_block`). A test runs the real `generate()` for both types, signed and ticked, and the remote `submitSignature` end to end.
2. **Linear scanning.** The `transform:...scale(0` check is one pass; `applyAcks` closes a tag by a backward walk. Timing tests: 200k repeated `transform:`, 1 MB of white space inside an `<input>` tag.
3. **Tags are an allowlist.** Unknown tags and attributes fail (`details`, `dialog`, `select`, `font`, `a`, event handlers); `no-print` is allowed only on the one print-button wrapper; text inside `no-print`, `nbd-sig-controls` or `nbd-sig-state` is not wording; only the widget script (no body, this site's `/pro/js/signature-widget.js`) is allowed; `<title/>`, `<style/>`, `<script/>` open raw elements; `textarea` and similar are raw; a control counts only when it is a live tag (not in a comment, textarea, noscript or template); nesting deeper than 400 fails.
4. **Deal mint, flag off.** The attic block reads no lead of its own; it uses the lead the mint already read, so flag-off minting is as on main.
5. **In-person, flag unreadable.** Still not refused (flag-off behaviour kept), but a contract-class or attic row is marked `atticGateUnverified` (server-written, rules-guarded like `evidenceRefused`) and the lead gets an `attic_gate_unverified` activity note.
6. **Estimate binding.** The generator writes `estimateId` on an attic form's row; `formBasis` binds to it when it is one of this lead's estimates (else the primary). The attic panel passes the lead's primary estimate; a form for another estimate is made by passing `estimateId` to `generate()` (no chooser in the panel yet).
7. **Remote, over the cap.** A signed copy over 2 MB now gets the too-large message, not "tick every box".

## Fourth review fixes, 2026-11-18 (suite `tests/attic-ack-review4-2026-11-18.test.js`)

1. (High) The rep-supplied page style block is no longer a property allowlist. Its rules must equal the generator's own (`PAGE_CSS_CANON` in `functions/attic-gate.js`, with colours and font stacks abstracted), so a rep can change only the tenant's brand colours and fonts, and those colours must stay readable (contrast of 3 or more against their own and the page background; the page stays light). `*{color:#fff;background:#fff}`, `font-size:1px`, `opacity:.01`, `left:-99vw`, `top:99999px` and `color:rgba(0,0,0,0)` are refused. If the generator's page CSS changes, update the canon (the suite builds a real form and fails until you do). Residual: a page colour that is readable on its own surface but is moved into a different dark surface by rearranging classes is not caught.
2. (High) A raw element (title, style, script, textarea ...) now ends at `</name` followed by white space, `/` or `>`, as in a browser. `<title>x</titlez>` no longer ends the title.
3. (Medium) A class, style, id or hidden attribute value containing `&` is refused (`entity_attr`), so `class="&#110;o-print"` cannot hide wording in print.
4. (Medium) The attic form and the gate are bound to the lead's highest-scope estimate (ties: the one being sent, then the primary). An estimateId that is not on THIS lead (and tenant) is refused: `decide` returns a closed gate (deal path, remote createSignRequest, in-person contracts) and `formBasis` throws `estimate_not_on_lead` (in-person attic form refuses; remote submitSignature returns 422 before the burn). This replaces the old "ignore a foreign estimate" behaviour.
5. (Low) More than 20,000 tags fails closed (`too_many_tags`; the scan stops at the cap), and a large stored copy is scanned once per request (small memo).
6. Rules test: a rep and an owner cannot create, plant, rewrite or remove `atticGateUnverified` (added to `tests/firestore-rules.attic-ack-2026-11-18.test.js`). Not run here: the emulator starts but `@firebase/rules-unit-testing` is not installed in this sandbox.

## Round 4 re-review: structural assessment and fixes, 2026-11-19

Structural compare (server regenerates the form from slots) was assessed and NOT built: the form is rendered in the browser (`document-generator-library.js` + `-templates.js` + the signature widget), while `functions/attic-ack.js` holds only the wording and two CSS constants. The letterhead, footer, badges, thumbnails, priced lines and signature blocks depend on the tenant profile, photos, conditions and signers, so a server template would be a second ~600-line renderer to keep byte-equal with the first. Instead the scanner is now position-based:

- `POSITIONS`: each generator class is valid only on its own element and inside its own surface (a `letterhead-contact` outside `.letterhead`, an `aa-p` on a div: `bad_position`). Text inside `.letterhead`, `.footer` and `.print-btn` is never the packet's wording (white-on-dark inheritance).
- Inline style: only the generator's `margin-top:36px;` on `div.section`, at most twice. No other margin, so nested margins cannot accumulate.
- Fonts: an allowlist of registered brand faces (`BRAND_FONTS`; a tenant face outside it is refused until added) in the exact stack shape.
- Colours: the page background must be near white, the server's fixed captions must read on it (3:1), and all text colours and light backgrounds of the usable rules must read against each other (4.5:1).
- Estimates: only LIVE estimates count toward the 20 cap (the read takes up to 101). A capped lead is treated as a re-roof, a signed form does not satisfy it, and the owner's override (bound to the basis) does.

## Round 5 re-review fixes, 2026-11-19

- `.letterhead` must be the first rendered child of `.doc-page` and `.footer` a direct child, once each, with nothing rendered after the footer (an empty or mid-page footer, or a nested letterhead, is `bad_position`). A style block or script renders nothing and does not count.
- The font-stack check now runs on the collapsed CSS and counts every `font-family\s*:` (white space, tab or newline before the colon no longer bypasses it).
- A required control with a hidden-class ancestor (`nbd-sig-controls`, `nbd-sig-state`, `no-print`, letterhead, footer, print button) is `hidden_control`.
- Pairing: each required control must sit in the label of its own packet paragraph (`pairing:<id>`), so swapped or moved controls are refused. Direct parents are required where the generator emits one (letterhead-brand, letterhead-contact, logo, aa-opt in tr, aa-row in section, affiliate cards, footer-brand, nbd-sig-state).
- Accepted residual (owner-only, visible in dry runs): EXTRA `aa-p` wording that is not in the canonical list is not refused, because the form's own paragraphs (conditions, photos, recommendation lines, dates) are dynamic. The required wording and its controls are pinned; extra visible wording can only add text a homeowner can read.
