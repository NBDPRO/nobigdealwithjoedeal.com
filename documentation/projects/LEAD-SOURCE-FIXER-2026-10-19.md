# Where customers came from - confirmed source (2026-10-19)

**Why:** the source stamped on a lead at intake misattributes badly, so CRM `lead_sources` counts the wrong things. Jo judges marketing by collected dollars per ad dollar using his own answers (his count: Yelp 61% of collected, Thumbtack paid 14%, Thumbtack direct 11%, referral/repeat 12%, door knock / Google / manufacturer locator one each). This tool lets the owner record that answer per customer and reports collected money by it. Ships **dark**.

**Flag:** `leadSourceFixer` in `feature_flags/_default` (or per user in `feature_flags/{uid}`). Absent or false = no button, nothing renders, no reads, no writes. Jo flips it in the Firebase console (Claude cannot write prod flags); reload the CRM afterwards (flags are cached up to 10 minutes). Same pattern as `bulkCloseStaleLeads` ([BULK-CLOSE-STALE-LEADS-2026-10-19](BULK-CLOSE-STALE-LEADS-2026-10-19.md)).

**The field (on `leads/{id}`):** `confirmedSource` is one of `yelp`, `thumbtack_paid`, `thumbtack_direct`, `referral`, `repeat`, `door_knock`, `google`, `manufacturer_locator`, `other`; plus `confirmedSourceNote` (optional text, 300 chars at most), `confirmedSourceBy` (uid) and `confirmedSourceAt` (server time). Never inferred: the intake source is shown on the card as "a hint only" and is never copied into the answer, and no script backfills anything.

**Who can write it (`firestore.rules`, `confirmedSourceWriteOk`):** platform admin, `company_admin` of the lead's company, or a solo operator (no role claim, own uid) who owns the lead. Managers, sales reps, viewers, other tenants and anonymous cannot, even though managers and reps can otherwise edit leads. Writes must be well-formed: a known value (or null to clear), a note of 300 chars at most, `confirmedSourceBy` equal to the caller, `confirmedSourceAt` equal to server time (no backdating). A lead create may not carry the fields. Saving a lead with the four fields unchanged is unaffected. Rule tests: `tests/firestore-rules.confirmed-source.test.js` (emulator, 30 checks, 4 rule mutations tried and caught).

**The screens (CRM board toolbar, button "Where customers came from", owner / company_admin only):**
- **Review:** one paying customer at a time, biggest collected dollars first. Card shows name, collected dollars, the intake source as a hint, nine 56px answer buttons, an optional note, **Skip for now** and, after an answer, **Undo** (writes a stamped null). Progress line: "N of M paying customers confirmed". "Paying" = collected money greater than zero, all time.
- **Report:** period menu (all time, last 12 months, this year, last 3 months). Per confirmed source: customers paid, collected dollars, share, ad spend, collected dollars per ad dollar. Customers without an answer have their own "Not confirmed yet" row and money on invoices with no customer has its own row, so the rows add up to the collected total. Under 480px each row is a card.

**Money rules:** collected = invoice payments minus refunds, dated by the day each payment arrived, through the one ledger `NBDRevenue.paymentsOf` (`docs/pro/js/collected-revenue.js`); integer cents throughout. Ad spend is the owner's monthly spend (`NBDNumbersData.loadSpend`, owner-only, entered in the Sunday review / catch-up screens): the `yelp` spend belongs to `yelp`, the `thumbtack` spend to `thumbtack_paid`. Other sources have no ad spend and show a dash, never a made-up ratio. If spend is not readable (not the account owner) the spend columns are dashes. The report writes nothing. No figures are published under `docs/`.

**No boot-time reads:** invoices and spend load only when the modal opens.

**Files:** `docs/pro/js/lead-source-fixer.js`, `docs/pro/css/lead-source-fixer.css`, `docs/pro/dashboard.html` (button + tags), `firestore.rules`, `tests/lead-source-fixer-2026-10-19.test.js` (72 checks, 10 mutations tried and caught), `tests/firestore-rules.confirmed-source.test.js` (+ a step in `.github/workflows/ci.yml`), `tests/ci-manifest.json`, `tests/dashboard-boot-budget-2026-10-03.test.js` (+1 deliberate script tag).

**Not done on purpose:** no production data touched, no prod flag written, the rules are not deployed by this PR beyond the normal deploy on merge. Possible follow-ups (not built): feed `confirmedSource` into the agent `lead_sources` tool and Reports > Lead Sources; show the answer on the customer page.

## Update 2026-10-22: confirmed source reaches the agent `lead_sources` tool

`lead_sources` (CoS / Dana / Frank / Theo) now returns `confirmed_sources` (leads and won per `confirmedSource` in the window) and `unconfirmed_leads` next to the unchanged intake-tag `sources`. Counts only: no money, no names. An unknown value or a cleared answer counts as unconfirmed. Collected dollars per confirmed source stay in the CRM report above. Test: `tests/lead-sources-confirmed-2026-10-22.test.js` (4 mutations tried, all caught). Why the intake tag undercounts Thumbtack: Thumbtack sends no hire event, and a customer who finds NBD on Thumbtack directly never arrives as a lead.
