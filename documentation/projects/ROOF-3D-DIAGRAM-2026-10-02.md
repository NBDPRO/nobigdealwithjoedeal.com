## Why Three.js, not Spline

Spline has no API or connector. Its scenes are hand-built in its editor,
which couldn't be driven reliably, and a Spline scene is a Three.js scene
under the hood anyway. So the 3D is built directly in Three.js:
- exact geometry, kept in the repo;
- no Spline runtime;
- testable.

If Jo ever designs a scene in Spline's editor, its export can replace the
model in `roof-3d.js`.

## Pieces

**Static diagram.** `docs/assets/images/roof-layers.svg`, about 27 KB:
- It is generated by `scripts/build-roof-diagram.js`. Edit the script, not
  the SVG; a test fails if the two drift.
- It is accessible (role img, title, desc) and has no script.
- Its airflow animation respects reduced motion.
- It is what no-JS visitors, screen readers and devices without WebGL get.

**3D view.** `docs/assets/js/roof-3d.js`, an ES module:
- **Nothing loads until "See it in 3D" is tapped.** The page's normal
  load, and the weekly page-speed watch, never pay for it.
- It builds a gable house: walls, gable ends with a vent, door and windows,
  fascia and soffits.
- The front roof plane is a cutaway staircase (finished on the left, bare
  deck on the right). The back plane is finished.
- **Controls:**
  - drag to turn it;
  - a slider pulls the layers apart;
  - tapping a legend item or a number badge highlights that part.
- **Behaviour:**
  - no wheel capture, and `touch-action: pan-y`, so the page still
    scrolls;
  - auto-spin stops on the first drag and never runs under reduced
    motion;
  - it renders on demand, only while on screen;
  - on stages narrower than 520 px, only the highlighted item's badge
    shows.

**Three.js** is vendored, because the CSP allows scripts from `'self'` only:
- The bundle is `docs/assets/js/vendor/three-roof.min.js`, Three.js r186
  trimmed to the 17 classes the viewer uses: 565 KB minified, 143 KB
  gzipped.
- The MIT license sits beside it.
- **Rebuild** with `scripts/build-three-roof.mjs`, which needs three and
  esbuild installed in a temp dir. Neither is a repo dependency.

**Tests.** `tests/roof-3d-2026-10-02.test.js`, 20 checks:
- page wiring, the legend, and the CODE tags;
- the SVG's accessibility, and that it matches its generator;
- the 3D view is tap-to-load, with no wheel capture and a reduced-motion
  path;
- the trimmed bundle exports every class `roof-3d.js` uses. This was
  break-tested: adding `T.Fog` fails the test and names `Fog`.

The 3D view was checked locally with the installed Chrome (Playwright
`executablePath`) on the hosting emulator, at desktop and phone width:
- no console errors;
- all badges placed;
- no sideways scroll.
