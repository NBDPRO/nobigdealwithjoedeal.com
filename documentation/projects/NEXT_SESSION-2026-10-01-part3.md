## §0 Read first

- **The appearance system is now four independent layers.** They combine
  freely, and each has its own file and test:
  1. **Colour theme.** 190+ registry entries in `theme-engine.js`, plus
     opt-in skins with scoped blocks in `theme-system.css`:
     - Live Ops;
     - Daylight, for full sun;
     - Jobsite;
     - the Duo two-colour pack.
  2. **Theme art.** Static, original SVG tiles per theme, generated by
     `scripts/build-theme-art.mjs` into `docs/pro/css/theme-art.css` (never
     hand-edit; `--check`).
     - Most canvas overlays skip phones, so before this most themes were
       colour swaps on Jo's iPhone.
     - Brand-named themes stay colour-only.
  3. **Shape & Depth.** `data-shape`. The new surface styles are in
     `css/shape-styles.css`: Clay, Liquid Glass, Skeuo, Spatial.
  4. **My Skin.** The user's own wallpaper, texture, mascot and accent, in
     `skins/{uid}/{slot}`.
     - Owner-only storage rules; read with `getBlob`, never a token URL.
     - Pictures are re-encoded on upload.
     - Erased with the account.

  product, because the repo is public and other tenants use it. My Skin is
  how Jo gets licensed or personal art on his own screen. 66 themes are named
  after franchises; as built-ins they stay colour-only.
