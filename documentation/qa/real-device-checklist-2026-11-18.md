# Real-device checklist: iPhone and printer (2026-11-18)

Everything in the [three-size format pass](three-size-format-pass-2026-11-18.md) used Chromium emulation and rendered PDFs. These are the things only a real iPhone (Safari) and a real printer can show. About 10 minutes. Tick, or write what you saw next to the line.

## On your iPhone (Safari, not the installed app, then once in the installed app)

1. **Lexington booking** (after the Lexington routing PR is live). Open `nobigdealwithjoedeal.com/areas/lexington-ky`, tap Book a free inspection, fill the form with a Lexington address (for example 100 Main St, Lexington, KY 40507), send. Pick My Time should open the **Lexington** calendar and the text should mention batched days. Then do it with a Cincinnati address: it should open the Cincinnati calendar.
2. **Invoice.** Open an invoice link from a text or email (or the portal, Your Documents, View). The header should not overlap, the line-item table should fit without sideways scroll, and Late Payment should be a full-width box.
3. **Customer estimate link.** Open it from a text: total, payment terms and the Print / Save PDF button all visible without pinch-zoom.
4. **Portal.** Open the portal link: progress bar, Review and sign button, Documents, message box. Tap the message box and check the keyboard does not hide the Send button.
5. **E-sign contract.** Open a signing link: read the contract, sign with a finger, check the date box. The signature pad must not scroll the page while you draw.
6. **Landscape.** Turn the phone sideways on the invoice and the contract: nothing cut off.

## On a printer (Letter, normal margins, "Actual size", not "Fit")

7. **E-sign contract PDF** (from the signed envelope or a test send): every page shows the footer and "Page n of N"; a long estimate repeats the Description / Qty / Amount headings on page 2; signature and date boxes sit inside the page.
8. **Invoice** and **receipt**: one or two pages, navy header and footer bars print (turn on background graphics if your browser asks), nothing cut at the edges.
9. **Thank-you letter**: exactly one page.
10. **Notice of Right to Cancel**: one page, signature line present, **no** Clear / Undo buttons.
11. **A guide PDF** (storm claim guide): page 1 cover, running header, last page back cover.

## What to send back

For any line that fails: the document, the device, and a photo or screenshot. A failing print is usually the printer's margins (check "Actual size" first).
