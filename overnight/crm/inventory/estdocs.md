### 3.4 Architecture

There are three to four pricing engines:
- per-SQ (estimate-builder-v2 calculatePerSq, integer cents)
- catalog/line-item (estimate-logic-engine resolveEstimate, **float dollars**, rounded once at the end)
- builder calculateLineItem (cents, no UI caller)
- classic (estimates.js, cents, config-only)

Job templates resolve through the logic engine. The same scope can differ by a cent between the float and cents engines, and by settings (ED-F05, ED-F06).
